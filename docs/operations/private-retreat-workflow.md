# Private Retreat Management Workflow

**Version:** 0.1 draft, 2026-07-24. First attempt for review; expect this to change.
**Applies to:** Private Infinite Leverage Retreats (1-2 founders on-site in Saigon, typically 5-6 days). Public retreats (Sydney, Melbourne) share phases 3-5 but have their own sales motion.
**Roles:** Dave (sales, delivery lead), Operations = My and Mai (logistics, finance, client comms), supporting engineers as rostered.

## The offer, so everyone sells the same thing

A private retreat is a founder (plus optionally a second guest) working alongside the Edge8 team in Saigon to ship real AI programs for their business. Reference pricing from past retreats:

- **Retreat fee:** $4,500-$10,000 depending on length and headcount
- **Add-on, Human Tokens pack:** ~$4,000
- **Add-on, Mac Mini (configured, theirs to keep):** ~$1,100
- Two formats: **Build** (ship a working program) vs **Process** (install a way of working). Position AI Officer vs AI Engineer expectations up front.
- Every retreat targets named projects completed by departure (e.g. janet.care, Atlas Dashboards, DOXA event). Projects completed is a tracked outcome, not a nice-to-have.

## Phase 1 · Scope and book (Dave, with Ops looped in)

Trigger: a qualified founder wants dates.

1. Agree dates, attendee count, format (Build vs Process), and the 2-3 named projects to complete.
2. Quote: retreat fee + add-ons (Human Tokens, Mac Mini). Confirm who pays commission if a referral is involved (e.g. referrer commission on an introduced client) and record it as a planned expense.
3. Send agreement and deposit invoice (QuickBooks).
4. **Ops creates the retreat in the admin:** event record (`type=retreat`, private, dates, capacity) the same day the deposit is paid.
5. **Ops enters the P&L estimate:** expected revenue lines (fee, add-ons) and estimated expenses (template below). This is the budget the retreat is managed against.

**Done when:** deposit paid, event record exists, estimated P&L entered, projects list written down.

## Phase 2 · Pre-retreat logistics (Ops, T-14 to arrival)

Standing checklist, owner per line:

| Item | Notes | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Visa support | Invitation letter or visa-on-arrival; last-minute visas have cost extra before, start at T-14 | Ops |
| Flights confirmed | Client books; we confirm arrival details | Ops |
| Accommodation | Serviced apartment (Lumier has worked well, ~2BR), book T-10 | Ops |
| Car and driver | Rental (nhieuxe.vn or Mr. Vu) for airport pickup + daily transport | Ops |
| Workspace | AIOpad room set: whiteboard, markers, prints, monitor, seating | Ops |
| Mac Mini decision | Buying: purchase and configure before arrival. Not buying: stage a loaner for the stay | Ops + Dave |
| Meeting room | External room if needed (e.g. The Hive) for kickoff or demo day | Ops |
| Staff roster | Who supports which days; days recorded per person for the P&L ($150/day flat) | Dave |
| Agenda with client | Day-by-day schedule agreed with the client the week before | Dave |
| F&B plan | Groceries, lunches, one welcome or closing dinner budgeted | Ops |

**Done when:** every line checked or consciously skipped, estimated P&L updated with real quotes.

## Phase 3 · Delivery (Dave leads, Ops supports daily)

Daily rhythm:

- Morning: standup against the projects list; adjust the day's plan.
- Build blocks with the roster staff; client works hands-on, not watching.
- **Expenses captured same day** into the retreat's P&L tab (actual amount, VND or USD as incurred, receipt photo to the shared folder). No end-of-retreat archaeology.
- End of day: one-line project status update to the client's stakeholders if agreed (this is part of the Human Tokens value).

**Done when:** each day closes with expenses logged and projects list updated.

## Phase 4 · Wrap (last day)

1. Demo day: walk through everything shipped against the projects list.
2. Mac Mini handover if purchased (configured, signed in, documented).
3. Enroll the client on the Claude certification path as the post-retreat continuation.
4. Ask for the testimonial while the win is fresh; ask who else should do this (referral).
5. Departure logistics: car to airport, apartment checkout.

**Done when:** projects demoed, handovers complete, testimonial and referral asked.

## Phase 5 · Close-out (Ops, within 7 days of departure)

1. Enter all remaining actuals in the P&L tab; mark every line's payment status (paid / to be paid).
2. Reconcile invoices in QuickBooks: fee, add-ons, any balance due.
3. Log staff days per person (drives the $150/day staff cost lines).
4. Review profit vs estimate with Dave; note the top variance drivers.
5. 15-minute retro: what to change in this document. Update this document.
6. CRM: log the retreat outcome on the client record; schedule the 30-day follow-up.

**Done when:** P&L complete with zero "to be paid" surprises, invoices reconciled, retro captured, follow-up scheduled.

## Standard P&L line template (private retreat)

**Revenue:** retreat fee · Human Tokens pack · Mac Mini · other.
**Expenses:** accommodation · staff cost (person x days x $150) · venue/meeting room · transportation (car, driver, fuel) · food & beverage · visa · equipment · commission · other.
