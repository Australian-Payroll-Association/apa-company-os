# Eight Edges intro video, script v7

Target length: 2:55. Audience: CEO of a 100 to 500 person company.
Structure: two-gaps hook, then introduce the Four Offices of the Future and tour each one with screen grabs of Edge8 OS supporting it. Human vs AI contrast stays as a one-liner inside each office.
All screens are live pages with real data; grab list per office below.

---

## 0:00–0:40 · The hook (two foundational gaps, with proof)

**Visual:** a strategy deck on a boardroom screen. Cut to the same office weeks later: everyone busy, heads down in a dozen different tools. On the Stanford line, full-screen stat card: 77% · DATA + WORKFLOW REDESIGN · STANFORD DIGITAL ECONOMY LAB.

**VO:**
You run a company of two hundred people. You know the strategy. But do the other one hundred ninety nine know how to execute it?

You want AI to help. Everyone does. But the reality is, AI in your company is handcuffed by two foundational gaps. Nobody has designed the workflows, so AI has no defined job. And your data is scattered across a dozen tools, so AI has nothing reliable to work from.

Don't take our word for it. Stanford studied fifty one successful enterprise AI programs. In seventy seven percent of them, the hardest work wasn't the technology. It was exactly this: data and workflow redesign.

## 0:40–1:05 · The Four Offices of the Future

**Visual:** the Edge8 OS sidebar. Four outcomes appear as words: greater revenue, higher performing talent, streamlined operations, innovative culture. Each one resolves into its office section: Revenue, Talent, Operations, Innovation.

**VO:**
So where do you start? Not with a re-org.

Every company strives to achieve four outcomes: greater revenue, higher performing talent, streamlined operations, and an innovative culture. We call this the Four Offices of the Future. It's a way to organize your work so AI can help, without re-organizing your business.

One company database underneath. Four offices on top. And in every workflow, a clear split between what AI does and what your people decide. Here's each office, live.

## 1:05–1:33 · Office of Revenue (generate revenue)

**Screen grabs:** the CRM company list (255 companies), the deal board mid-drag, one deal open with its full interaction history, then a call transcript dropped in and the branded proposal page appearing.

**VO:**
The Office of Revenue. Every contact, every company, every conversation in one place: nine hundred people, two hundred fifty five companies, and a deal board your whole team trusts.

Watch this: a sales call ends, the transcript goes in, and the AI updates the CRM, moves the deal, and drafts a proposal page, live, in under ten minutes. Your people do the call, the trust, and the price. The AI does everything after the handshake.

## 1:33–2:00 · Office of Talent (develop talent)

**Screen grabs:** a job req with its stack-ranked applications (318 across 57 roles, names blurred), one candidate open with AI score and summary beside the recruiter's own assessment, then the org chart and a 1-on-1 coaching prep page.

**VO:**
The Office of Talent. Three hundred eighteen applications in the system right now. The AI has read every resume against the job description, scored each candidate, and ranked the list. Your recruiter meets the top five and makes the call. Nobody gets hired by software.

And it doesn't stop at hiring: onboarding plans, the org chart, and every leader walking into their one-on-ones with an AI-prepared brief instead of a blank page.

## 2:00–2:22 · Office of Operations (streamline operations)

**Screen grabs:** the operations hub: time off board approving a request, the equipment register, a work request moving across the board, and the finance sync pulling invoices from the accounting system.

**VO:**
The Office of Operations. Leave requests, equipment, vendors, work requests: the unglamorous machinery that eats your managers' time. Here the AI does the tracking and the syncing, straight from the systems of record, so the numbers are never stale and nothing waits in an inbox. Your people approve, your people decide. The chasing is gone.

## 2:22–2:45 · Office of Innovation (innovate)

**Screen grabs:** the ideas feed with team submissions, the AI programs view, and the workflows library.

**VO:**
And the Office of Innovation, the one most companies never staff. Every idea from your team lands in one feed instead of dying in a chat thread. The best ones become designed workflows, and every workflow you saw today started here. This is where your company learns: people spot the opportunity, AI helps build it, and the system keeps it.

## 2:45–2:55 · Close

**Visual:** pull back to the full sidebar: four offices, one database. End card.

**VO:**
Four offices, one database, and one pattern everywhere: AI does the reading, the tracking, and the preparing. Your people do the judging, the deciding, and the relationships. We run our own company on this, everything you just saw is live. This is Edge8.
