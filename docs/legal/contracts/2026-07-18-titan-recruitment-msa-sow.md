## II. GENERAL PROVISIONS

1. **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated pursuant to
   Section IX ("Term"). Individual SOWs may specify their own duration.
2. **Modification.** Any changes to this Agreement or an SOW must be in writing and signed by both
   Parties.
3. **Permits and Licenses.** Each Party will maintain, at its own expense, all licenses and permits
   required with respect to its own business.

## III. SERVICES AND DELIVERY

1. **Statements of Work.** Edge8 will perform the services and deliver the deliverables described
   in each executed SOW. The initial SOWs are attached as **SOW 1 (AI Program Jumpstart)** and
   **SOW 2 (Guided Workflows — Human Token Packs)**.
2. **Client-Owned Infrastructure.** Deliverables will be built on accounts held in the Client's own
   name (including database hosting, web hosting, and AI model accounts — currently Supabase,
   Vercel, and Anthropic/Claude). The Client is responsible for the direct running costs of those
   accounts, which are billed by the respective vendors to the Client and are not Edge8 fees.
3. **Cooperation.** Client will provide timely access to the systems, data, personnel, and
   decisions reasonably required for Edge8 to perform the Services, including API or export access
   to Client's existing systems of record (currently RDB, and Mercury following migration).
4. **Personnel.** Edge8 performs the Services through its own team, including engineers engaged
   through Cong Ty Edge8 AI LTD, Edge8's registered entity in Vietnam. Edge8 remains responsible
   for its personnel and for the quality of the Services regardless of which Edge8 personnel
   perform them.
5. **Working Hours.** Edge8's engineering team operates on Vietnam working hours (GMT+7, Monday to
   Friday, observing Vietnam national holidays), with crossover meeting windows scheduled to
   overlap with Client's Perth business hours (GMT+8) `[REVIEW: near-identical timezones make this
   easy — confirm you want this clause at all, or simplify]`.
6. **Leadership Meetings.** Leadership from both Parties will meet at least once per month for
   thirty (30) minutes to review performance, goals, and scope.

## IX. TERMINATION

a. **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on thirty

   performed and deliverables completed through the termination date; (ii) prepaid but unused
   human token packs under SOW 2 are refunded at the pack rate `[REVIEW: proposal says tokens have

   (iii) the SOW 1 money-back gate in SOW 1 §4 is unaffected.
b. **Termination on Breach.** Either Party may terminate for material breach on written notice if
   the breach is not cured within thirty (30) calendar days of notice.
c. **Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other becomes
   insolvent, enters liquidation or administration, or ceases to conduct business.
d. **Post-Term.** Provisions that by their nature survive (confidentiality, ownership, payment of
   accrued fees, liability, non-solicitation) survive termination. On termination Edge8 will hand
   over all credentials, repositories, and documentation for the Client-owned systems in an orderly

e. **Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for one (1) year after, neither Party will solicit for
   employment the other Party's personnel without written consent; breach triggers a fee of twelve
   (12) months of that person's fully burdened compensation.

# SOW 1 — AI Program Jumpstart

Executed under the Master Services Agreement between Talent Edge LLC (d/b/a Edge8) and Titan
Recruitment dated ____________. Capitalized terms carry the meanings in the Agreement.

1. **Scope.** Over approximately eight (8) weeks from the SOW start date, Edge8 will:
   a. Map the data currently held in Client's RDB system and assess RDB's API/export quality;
   b. Stand up a database in Client's own Supabase account, modelled for recruitment (candidates,
      vacancies, placements, contractors, clients), seeded from Edge8's company database template;
   c. Build a one-way sync from RDB (or Mercury, if the migration timing makes that the better
      first source) into the Client-owned database;
   d. Deploy a secured, browser-based dashboard application (Next.js/React) on Client's own Vercel
      account with initial views of Client's live data;
   e. Build two (2) to four (4) automations agreed with Client during the engagement;
   f. Deliver a written **Phase I implementation plan** covering the guided-workflows roadmap
      (the "Phase I Plan").
2. **Out of scope.** Per-feature guided workflows (SOW 2), the Four Offices of the Future workshop,
   the Mercury migration itself, and ongoing managed services.
3. **Fees.** **AUD $15,000**, invoiced on execution of this SOW and payable up front before work
   begins.
4. **Money-back gate.** If, on delivery of the Phase I Plan, Client is not satisfied with it,
   Client may reject the Phase I Plan by written notice within fourteen (14) days of delivery
   `[REVIEW: proposal says "full refund if the Phase I plan does not convince you" with no window —
   a notice window protects Edge8; confirm 14 days and confirm whether rejection also terminates
   the SOW with Client keeping the work delivered to date]`. On rejection, Edge8 will refund the
   full AUD $15,000 within thirty (30) days.

# SOW 2 — Guided Workflows · Human Token Packs

Executed under the Master Services Agreement between Talent Edge LLC (d/b/a Edge8) and Titan
Recruitment dated ____________.

1. **Model.** Client purchases prepaid packs of forty (40) human tokens at **AUD $3,000 per pack**.
   One human token equals one hour of Edge8 expert time, leveraged with AI tooling. Tokens have no
   expiry and no monthly fee.
2. **Scoping.** On receipt of Client's scope of works, Edge8 will return a per-feature estimate in
   tokens within seven (7) days, including a plain statement of anything Edge8 believes is not
   worth building. Client chooses which features to commission and in what order. Work on a
   feature begins only after Client approves its estimate in writing (email sufficient).
3. **Transparency.** Edge8 will maintain a tracker visible to Client showing tokens consumed, time
   spent, AI usage, and pull requests shipped.
4. **Estimates.** Per-feature token estimates are good-faith estimates, not fixed prices. If Edge8
   expects a feature to exceed its estimate by more than twenty percent (20%), Edge8 will pause and
   seek Client's written approval before continuing `[REVIEW: overrun mechanics are not in the
   proposal — confirm the 20% pause threshold]`.
5. **AI usage costs.** Claude API usage is billed by Anthropic directly to Client's own account and
   is separate from token packs.
6. **Delivery.** Each feature drops into the dashboard shell delivered under SOW 1 and becomes
   Client property per Agreement §VI on payment.
