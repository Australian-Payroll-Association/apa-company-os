## 2. Service delivery model — the core structural change

| | Original | New |
|---|---|---|
| Employer of record | Remofirst **subcontracts** to a network of vetted third-party "Partners" in each country; Remofirst itself "will at no time act as the employer of record" | **Edge8 is the direct employer of record itself**, through Cong Ty Edge8 AI LTD in Vietnam — no subcontracted Partner network |
| Recitals | Frame Remofirst as a platform connecting clients to a Partner network "regardless of where [Talent] may be located in the world" | Reframed around Edge8 employing Talent directly in Vietnam |
| Partner vetting clause (III.2.b–c) | Remofirst warrants its Partners are vetted and binds them to matching contract terms | **Removed** — replaced with a direct warranty that Edge8 itself holds the licenses/registrations to act as EOR in Vietnam |
| IP-assignment Partner clause (VI.h) | Remofirst binds Partners to the same IP-assignment terms | **Removed** — no Partners to bind |
| Non-circumvention of Partners (X.e.4) | Client barred from independently engaging Remofirst's Partners for 12 months post-termination | **Removed** — not applicable without a Partner network. The separate mutual employee non-solicitation clause was kept. |
| Data-sharing / User Content clauses (XIV.c, XIV.m) | Reference sharing data with / exchanging content through "Partner(s)" | Partner references stripped |

**Why it matters:** this is not a find-and-replace of "Remofirst" → "Edge8." The original agreement's entire liability posture assumes a third party (the Partner) is the actual employer taking on local labor-law risk, with Remofirst as an intermediary platform. The new agreement makes Edge8 itself the employer of record, which is a materially different risk position — Edge8 (via Cong Ty Edge8 AI LTD) now carries the Vietnamese labor-law compliance obligation directly rather than warranting a third party's compliance.

## 8. Everything left structurally unchanged

The following sections carried over with only "Remofirst" → "Edge8" name substitution, no
structural edits: Term/Modification/Permits (II.1–3), the Platform/Terms-of-Use clause (II.4, now
pointing at edge8.ai/legal/eula), KYB due diligence (II.5), Confidentiality (V), IP Ownership (VI,
aside from the Partner-binding clause removed), Limitation of Liability & Indemnification (VII),
Warranties (IX), the four Termination triggers and post-term obligations other than the
Partner-circumvention clause (X), Force Majeure (XIII), and the remaining Miscellaneous Provisions
(XIV) — Proprietary Rights, Waiver, Severability, Entire Agreement, Headings, Counterparts,
Successors, Third Party Beneficiaries, Language, and Data Consent.
