Edge8 Company OS

The data model of the company_os schema, after the brand / legal-entity dimension was retired. One Postgres database running CRM, recruiting, HR, time-off, commerce, content and an AI-app platform — now hung off a single people-centric spine.

01

The backbone

The schema now has one gravitational center. people is referenced by 57 of the 97 tables; team_members is the internal-staff spine for HR, Time Off and Recruiting. Everything else plugs into these.

Hub-and-spoke, minus the org layer. A person is the same row whether they appear as a CRM lead, a job candidate, a customer on an order, or a team member on leave. The old legal_entities → brands org layer is gone from the graph.
Two tables are now islands. brands · 5 rows · 0 refs legal_entities · 3 rows · 0 refs Both were kept but every brand_id / legal_entity_id was dropped, so nothing points at them anymore.
02

How the domains attach

Each curve is one or more foreign keys from a domain into a backbone table — thicker means more links. With brand and legal-entity gone, almost everything routes through people, while Time Off and HR route through team_members.
