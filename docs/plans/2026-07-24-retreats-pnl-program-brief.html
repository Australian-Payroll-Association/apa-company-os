AI Program Brief · 5Ds

Infinite Leverage Retreats P&L

Move retreat profit-and-loss out of a fragile Excel workbook and onto the Events module in the Edge8 admin, with a confidential, history-preserving home for employee wages that only Dave and Mai can see.

Outcome: Cheaper Operations Secondary: Increased Revenue visibility Type: Admin module extension Difficulty: Medium
Owner: Dave Hajdu Users: My & Mai (Operations) Date: 2026-07-24 Workflow: Retreat P&L + wage records
D1

Definition of the Problem

Who · Cost · Why now · Success
  • Who Operations (My and Mai), who run the P&L for every Private and Public Infinite Leverage Retreat, and Dave, who needs live margin per retreat instead of after-the-fact accounting.
  • Cost Each retreat's profit is computed by hand in a shared workbook: one tab per retreat, hand-typed rate formulas, VND and USD mixed line by line, Stripe payments reconciled from notes, and a Summary tab that is retyped rather than computed. Close-out takes roughly half a day per retreat, and that cost repeats and grows with every retreat the program adds.
  • Why now Retreats are a fast-growing program: four are already booked across August and September, and today every one is run by hand in a shared workbook. Left as-is, each new retreat pulls more of the operations team's time into spreadsheet work. Putting a repeatable system around it now keeps that time on delivering retreats, not accounting for them.
  • Success Every retreat from Sydney (Aug 27) onward is managed entirely in the admin app with profit visible the day the retreat ends, and every employee wage lives in a controlled record, dual currency, full history, visible to exactly two people.

Diagram & Documented Workflow

Two workstreams, one system
Workstream A · Retreat P&L on the Events module
Ops enters linesRevenue & expense line items per retreat, estimated vs actual, VND or USD
Stripe auto-revenuePaid registrations appear as read-only revenue rows, no retyping
↓
P&L tab on the retreatGrouped by classification: accommodation, staff, venue, transport, F&B, visa, commission. Totals normalized to USD
↓
Live profitTotal revenue, total expense, profit/(loss), estimated and actual, the day the retreat ends
  • One new table holds the P&L lines, attached to the existing retreat event record. Nothing is duplicated from Stripe or QuickBooks.
  • Staff cost lines are person + days at a flat $150/day (overridable). Real wages never appear in the P&L, by design.
  • Estimated vs Actual vs Difference per line, with payment status (unpaid, to be paid, paid), matching how the Sydney and Melbourne tabs work today.
Workstream B · Confidential wage records
Dave / Mai onlyCompensation section on each employee record. Other admins never fetch the data
Dual currencySalary stored in VND and USD, converted at a fixed 25,500, both fields editable
Full historyEvery change adds a new dated row with reason and approver. Nothing is overwritten
Access rule: wages and all PII (ID documents, bank details, personal contacts) are gated to Dave and Mai only. Being an admin is not enough; the check happens server-side and the data is never sent to anyone else's browser. Wage data is also invisible to the admin AI assistant.
D4

ROI Determined

FAST goal + measures
FAST goal

By 4 weeks after launch, every retreat from Sydney (Aug 27) onward has its P&L maintained entirely in the admin app, the workbook is retired, and profit per retreat is visible the day the retreat ends.

Close-out effort: ~4 hours → under 1 hour per retreat
  • 100% of retreats (6 scheduled + backfilled history) have a live P&L; summary numbers are computed, never retyped.
  • Zero manual Stripe reconciliation. Paid registrations flow in automatically.
  • 100% of active staff have a wage record with history; access verified restricted to exactly 2 people.
  • Handover-proof. The process survives the workbook owner leaving, because the process is the product.
D5

Deployment Plan

6 phases · first action in 7 days
  1. Database migration. P&L lines table, dual-currency wage columns, the Dave-and-Mai-only access flag. Verified with a smoke test before anything is built on top.
  2. Data layer + access gate. Server-side accessors; verified that a non-cleared admin gets no wage data at all.
  3. P&L tab on the retreat detail page. Verified by type-check and production build.
  4. Compensation section on the employee record, rendered only for Dave and Mai.
  5. Backfills. Historical retreats imported from the workbook (totals must match per retreat); salaries imported from MasterList at 25,500.
  6. Handover. My and Mai run the Sydney retreat P&L in the app; the workbook goes read-only.
Definition of Done
  1. My and Mai can create, edit, and delete revenue and expense lines on any retreat, with estimated vs actual, VND or USD per line, and USD totals and profit computed automatically.
  2. Stripe-paid registrations appear automatically as read-only revenue rows on public retreats.
  3. Staff lines compute at $150/day from person + days, overridable.
  4. All historical retreats exist in the system and each backfilled retreat's totals match the workbook.
  5. Every active employee has a current salary row in both VND and USD (25,500 rate), and every change creates a new dated row so history is never lost.
  6. Wage and PII surfaces return data only for Dave and Mai, verified by loading them as another admin and confirming nothing is fetched.
  7. No salary value appears in the repo, logs, or commit history.
  8. Type-check and production build pass; PR reviewed and merged by Dave.
  9. One real retreat (Sydney, Aug 27) is closed out entirely in the app and the workbook is retired.
Out of scope for v1: fixed program expenses and all-up program P&L · summary dashboard (revenue by stream, participants vs the 100 goal) · To Buy procurement list · QuickBooks reconciliation of retreat expenses · automatic Stripe fee capture · wage-based retreat costing.
