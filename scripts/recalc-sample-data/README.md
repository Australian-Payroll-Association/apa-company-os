# Recalc sample workbook

Generated by scripts/generate-recalc-sample-data.ts against the real MA000019 rule set (supabase/03-recalc-ma000019-ruleset.sql). 20 employees spanning every employment type (full-time/part-time/casual x day/shift workers), leave, every allowance type, a call-back, weekend/public-holiday work, and one junior (17yo) employee. One pay period, one sample public holiday.

Upload pay-review-sample.xlsx at /admin/innovation/recalc. Everything nets to ~$0 EXCEPT these deliberately seeded discrepancies:

- E01, ordinary: ordinary pay short by $50 (data-entry error) (expected 2049.70, paid 1999.70)
- E03, ordinary: part-time ordinary pay over by $15 (rounding difference) (expected 1178.00, paid 1193.00)
- E07, afternoon_permanent: afternoon shift loading paid at the non-permanent rate, not the permanent-shiftworker rate (expected 1104.32, paid 1024.32)
- E11, annual_leave_loading: annual leave loading underpaid — paid roughly half of what's owed (expected 35.87, paid 17.94)
- E12, first_aid_allowance: first aid allowance overpaid by one extra week (duplicate payment) (expected 41.18, paid 61.77)
- E14, higher_duties_allowance: higher duties differential not paid at all (expected 122.35, paid 0.00)
- E17, overtime_saturday_outside_hours: Saturday overtime underpaid by $30 (expected 824.60, paid 794.60)
- E18, sunday_penalty: Sunday penalty underpaid — paid at roughly 80% of the correct rate (expected 373.32, paid 298.66)
- E19, public_holiday_penalty: public holiday work overpaid by $40 (double-counted top-up) (expected 269.72, paid 309.72)
- E20, ordinary: junior casual rate applied incorrectly, short by $5 (expected 212.43, paid 207.43)
