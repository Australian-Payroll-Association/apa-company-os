01The problem

Nobody in the building can lead it

95%

of enterprise AI pilots deliver no measurable return.

MIT Sloan

The tools are available to everyone at the same price. The three roles that turn them into results are on nobody's payroll.

  • Missing

    Workflow designer

    Nobody whose job is to write down how the work actually happens, then redesign it so an agent can carry part of it.

  • Missing

    Database designer

    Nobody who can model the company into a schema an agent can read. The data stays trapped in ten tools that do not talk.

  • Missing

    AI program lead

    Nobody accountable for the whole thing landing. It is a side project for someone who already has a full time job.

And you cannot recruit your way out. These titles barely existed three years ago, so almost nobody on the market has done it twice. You build the people, or you borrow one.

03The solution

Build the people, or borrow one
