AI Interview Panelist

Every interview transcript flows into the applicant tracking system, and AI takes a permanent seat on every interview panel: scoring with evidence, remembering across rounds, and never casting the deciding vote.

Why

The highest-signal stage leaves no record

Hiring already runs through the Company OS ATS. The resume stage has an AI pass: every application gets an AI summary and rating before a human looks at it. But the interviews, where the real signal lives, leave nothing behind. The database tables built for them (interviews, panel seats, scorecards, per-criterion scores) exist and sit empty. Transcripts from Lark Minutes end up in a Downloads folder and die there.

Live example. Dang Nguyen, AI Engineer candidate, went through three rounds: Mai (recruiter screen), Khoa (engineering), Dave (founder). None of the three exists anywhere in the system. The only artifacts are a rating column from the resume screen and a transcript file on a laptop.

The fix extends a pattern that already works. The AI resume screen becomes an AI presence one stage deeper: a panelist in every interview round.

Blind-firstThe AI scorecard stays hidden until every human on that round has submitted. No anchoring.
AI advises, never decidesThe AI verdict is one voice on the panel. Humans move the candidate forward or out.

How a round flows

Transcript in, scorecards out

  1. Transcript arrives. Paste or upload on the application page. Lark Minutes exports work as-is. Auto-pull from Lark comes later.
  2. Parse and match. Date, duration, and speakers are read from the file; the round record links it to the candidate's application.
  3. AI panelist reviews. Claude reads the transcript plus the job description, the resume, the earlier AI screen, the company core values, and every prior round's transcript and scorecards.
  4. Scorecard written. Recommendation, overall score, per-criterion scores. Every score cites verbatim quotes with timestamps. Unverified claims are flagged, and suggested questions for the next round are attached.
  5. Panel compares. Once the humans have submitted, all scorecards render side by side. Gaps of a full point on any criterion are highlighted for discussion.

Transcript quality is handled, not trusted. Speech recognition on these calls is rough (today's transcript renders "Claude Code" as "clock code"). The panelist flags low-confidence quotes and is instructed never to hold garbled transcription against the candidate.

Carry-forward is the compounding part. After each round the AI writes what was verified, what is still open, and what the next interviewer should probe. Three rounds stop being three disconnected opinions and become one thread of evidence.

Build plan

Three PRs, no schema changes

The database already has every table this needs. The AI call copies the pattern already running in the resume screen. All UI lands on the existing application manage page in Edge8 OS admin.

PR 1

Rounds and transcripts

WHAT IT DOES

Adds an interview journey to the application page: create rounds, seat panelists (the AI seat appears automatically), paste or upload a transcript per round, and a quick scorecard form for humans.

DONE WHEN

Dang's three rounds exist with the right panels, today's founder transcript is readable in admin, and a human scorecard can be submitted.

PR 2

The AI panelist

WHAT IT DOES

On transcript ingest, Claude writes the AI seat's scorecard: evidence-cited scores per criterion, recommendation, and the carry-forward block. Blind-first reveal and disagreement highlighting.

DONE WHEN

The AI scorecard for Dang's founder round generates from today's transcript, stays hidden until Dave submits his, then renders side by side with disagreements flagged.

PR 3

Round plans per role

WHAT IT DOES

Each role defines its interview plan: ordered rounds, each with the rubric that round scores. Round creation pre-fills from the plan, and the AI scores against the round's rubric (a recruiter screen scores motivation and communication; a founder round scores values fit).

DONE WHEN

Creating a round for a new AI Engineer candidate pre-fills title and rubric, and the AI scorecard's criteria match the plan.

After PR 2, Dang's journey is backfilled end to end and becomes the working prototype.

Open questions

Out of scope for now

  • Lark Minutes auto-pull (production has no Lark app credentials yet; ingestion starts manual).
  • Panel analytics: whether AI scores predict probation outcomes better than human ones. Needs months of data.
  • Feeding the role's candidate ranking from interview scorecards.
