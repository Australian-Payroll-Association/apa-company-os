01 · The idea

Show the real system, daily

One Overview film (3 to 4 minutes) tells the whole story: how strategy becomes goals, goals become metrics, metrics get reviewed weekly, and problems become issues that get solved. Then a daily series, one feature per episode, 60 to 90 seconds each, posted on a steady cadence.

Every episode follows the same skeleton so production is repeatable:

  1. Cold open on the UI (0 to 5s): the feature already on screen, mid-action. No logo intro.
  2. Hook (one line): the pain this feature kills.
  3. Three beats (15 to 25s each): three things you can do, shown live in the product.
  4. Close (5 to 10s): one-line takeaway plus a soft CTA ("Full series in the playlist. Built by Edge8.").

Format: 16:9 landscape, 1080p, for YouTube and the LinkedIn feed. No Shorts for now. If Shorts come later, the same Playwright specs re-render at 9:16 with tighter zooms; nothing is re-shot.

02 · The pipeline

AI does the recording, you do the approving

No human screen-records anything. The whole episode renders end to end from a script file:

1 · Demo tenant
A seeded company in 8 Edges with realistic fake people, deals, goals, and boards. No client data ever on screen.
2 · Playwright flow
One spec per episode logs in, drives the scripted clicks, and records 1080p video. Re-run to re-record after any UI change.
3 · AI voiceover
ElevenLabs reads the approved script. One consistent voice across the series.
4 · Assembly
ffmpeg lays VO over the capture, adds title card, captions, and end card. Descript as manual fallback for fine cuts.
5 · Your QC
You watch the render, approve or request a change, then post. The only human step.

Timing sync

Each script beat carries a rough duration. The Playwright spec paces its actions to those durations (deliberate cursor moves, short holds on the payoff frame), so VO and screen stay aligned without manual editing. Where drift happens, ffmpeg trims the longer side at beat boundaries.

Where avatar tools fit

Synthesia or HeyGen can supply a 10-second talking-head intro and outro for the Overview film only. The daily episodes stay pure product plus voiceover: faster to make, and the product is the star. Higgsfield and similar generative video tools are not a fit here, they invent footage, and we want the real UI.

03 · Prerequisites

What has to exist before episode one

  • Demo tenant: a seeded company ("Northwind Manufacturing" or similar) with 8 to 10 fake people, 3 active deals, a full goal tree, populated metrics with history, a weekly sync with items, open issues, one client roadmap, one task board mid-sprint, and an ATS req with candidates across stages.
  • Demo login: a dedicated demo admin account, scoped to the demo tenant.
  • Recording rig: a Playwright project in the repo (specs, fixtures, video config), plus an ElevenLabs voice pick and an ffmpeg assembly script.
  • Brand kit: title card and end card templates (navy, mint, Manrope), caption style, thumbnail template.

Brand rules apply on screen and in copy: "Edge8" is never written in all caps, and no em dashes anywhere, including captions, titles, and descriptions.

E00 · LAUNCH

Overview: the whole system in four minutes

3 to 4 minutes · optional avatar intro and outro
Voiceover

Most companies run on two disconnected things: a strategy deck nobody opens, and a pile of tools nobody reconciles. 8 Edges is what happens when you connect them.

It starts at the top. The Company Dashboard shows the whole business on one screen: goals, the metrics behind them, and the issues in the way. Nothing here is typed in by hand, it all flows up from work happening below.

Goals cascade. The company goal breaks into team goals, team goals into personal ones, so every person can trace their week to the mission. Each goal is wired to metrics, real numbers with real history, updated on schedule, not the night before the board meeting.

Once a week the leadership team runs a Sync inside the system. Same agenda every week: review the scorecard, review the goals, surface issues. Anything off track becomes an issue with an owner and a deadline, and it stays on the list until it is actually solved.

Then the offices. Revenue runs the CRM, where a sales call becomes a live proposal in minutes. Talent runs hiring, from pipeline to interview scorecards to coaching once they join. Operations keeps the machine healthy, down to grading every laptop in the fleet. And everything execution-shaped lands on task boards shared by the team, by clients, and by AI agents that file and move cards alongside the humans.

Clients see their slice through their own portal: a live roadmap, real progress, no status meetings.

That's 8 Edges. Strategy at the top, execution at the bottom, and nothing lost in between. This month we're posting a short demo every day, one feature at a time. Follow along.

Company Dashboard

60 to 90 seconds
Voiceover

If you asked five people in your company how the business is doing, you'd get five answers. This screen exists so there's only one.

This is the Company Dashboard in 8 Edges. One screen, the whole company: the goals you committed to, the metrics that prove progress, and the issues standing in the way.

06 · Production and posting

Cadence and checklist

Build order

  1. Seed the demo tenant and demo login → verify every episode's flow has real-looking data
  2. Build the recording rig (Playwright config, ffmpeg assembly, ElevenLabs voice pick) → render a test episode end to end
  3. Batch-record the first arc (E01 to E06) → your QC pass on all six
  4. Record E00 Overview last, once the episode style is settled → optional avatar intro
  5. Launch: post E00, then one episode per weekday

Posting conventions

  • YouTube: one playlist ("8 Edges, daily"), numbered titles ("8 Edges E04: The Weekly Sync"), consistent thumbnail template, chapters on E00.
  • LinkedIn: native upload (not a YouTube link) for reach, 2 to 3 line post in Dave's voice, first line is the hook from the script.
  • Captions: burned-in, since LinkedIn autoplays muted.
  • Per-episode QC: no client data on screen, brand casing correct, no em dashes in captions or titles, VO and screen in sync at each beat.

Effort estimate: the demo tenant and recording rig are the real work, roughly a few focused sessions. After that, each episode is: approve script, run pipeline, QC 90 seconds of video. The daily cadence costs minutes per day, not hours.
