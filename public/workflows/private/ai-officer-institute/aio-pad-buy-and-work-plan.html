Who does what

Roles

  • V — Viha, remote. Design decisions, artist brief, five approvals, sign-off
  • A — Admin, on site. Orders, payments, deliveries, supervises trades, positions everything, sends approval photos
  • T — Trades. Painter 2 days · installer 2 visits

Do not change

  • Bedding — T400 cotton, white, no logo. Not 600TC
  • Balcony chairs — aluminium or 304 stainless, not painted steel
  • Bulbs — 2700K dimmable, every lamp
  • Rugs — flatweave, washable. No jute under the dining table
  • Fixings — painted plasterboard only
  • Artwork — one artist, six paintings, one batch. Bathrooms are prints

Payments and handover

Payments

  • Day 1 — deposits 30–50%: Sagatex, armchair, Minh Thy, TVs
  • Day 3–4 — artist sketch fee, then commission deposit
  • Day 4 — shopping run, keep every receipt
  • Day 6 — painter, after daylight inspection
  • Day 9 — Mia Home · Day 10 — installer visit 1
  • Day 21 — artist balance · Day 28 — framer · Day 29 — installer visit 2
  • Hold 10% of the trades bill until after day 30

Handover check · day 30

  • Frames level, centres 150cm, no visible fixings
  • TV cabling concealed
  • Every lamp 2700K and working
  • Both beds identical, fold straight
  • Dispensers filled, towels rolled, robes hung
  • Reset sheet in a cupboard in each room
