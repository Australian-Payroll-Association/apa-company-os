Who does what

Roles

  • V — Viha, remote. Design decisions, artist brief, the five approvals, final sign-off. 8 items.
  • A — Admin, on site. Places orders, pays deposits, receives deliveries, supervises trades, positions everything, sends approval photos. 49 items.
  • T — Trades. Painter 2 days. Installer 2 visits. Booked and supervised by Admin.

Specs Admin must not change

  • Bedding — T400 cotton, plain white, no logo. Not 600TC
  • Balcony chairs — aluminium or 304 stainless. Not painted steel
  • Bulbs — every lamp 2700K dimmable
  • Rugs — flatweave, washable. No jute under the dining table
  • Fixings — painted plasterboard only. Never stone, tile, marble or joinery
  • Artwork — one artist, five pieces, one batch. No originals in the bathrooms

Priority

Start with Priority 1. Bedding is the one thing a guest touches for eight hours, and a bare mattress is what makes the apartment read as vacant. It sits with the two longest leads in the plan — the artist at 2–3 weeks and the armchair at 7–14 days — plus the paint, because nothing can be hung until it is dry.

Payments

  • Day 1 — deposits 30–50%: Sagatex, armchair, Minh Thy, TVs
  • Day 3 — artist sketch fee, then commission deposit on day 4
  • Day 4 — shopping run paid on the day, every receipt kept
  • Day 6 — painter, after inspection in daylight
  • Day 9 — Mia Home, three printed pieces
  • Day 10 — installer, first visit
  • Day 21 — artist balance on approval of the paintings
  • Day 28 — framer on collection · Day 29 — installer, second visit
  • Hold 10% of the trades bill until after day 30

Handover check · day 30

  • Frames level, centres 150cm, no visible fixings
  • TV cabling concealed
  • Every lamp 2700K and working
  • Both beds identical, fold straight
  • Dispensers filled, towels rolled, robes hung
