UX Questions

AI Officer — what needs decided

14 Aug 2026 · from a live walkthrough of aiolabz.com

Student view

What the learner sees, gets and does.

  • How many missions and how many electives does certification actually take? The page says "missions, micro sessions and challenges" but never a number.
  • What do I receive when I finish — a PDF, an image, or a web page?
  • Whose name goes on it, when the product currently lets me exist without one?
  • Can I prove to an employer that it is real?
  • Where can I share it, and can I edit the text before it posts?
  • Does one minute of video count as "attended" for a micro session?
  • What does my progress percentage include — this track, or everything I'm enrolled in?
  • When am I told "you're all caught up", and is it true?
  • When a button is greyed out, what am I told about why?
  • What happens when my session expires in the middle of a task?
  • What do I see when a recording isn't published yet?
  • On the onboarding screen, can I actually choose my track?

Build view

Never seen directly, but decides everything above.

  • Where does the certificate template live, and who owns it?
  • Which platforms can we actually auto-post to, and which are download-and-post-yourself?
