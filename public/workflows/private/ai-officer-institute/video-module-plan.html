Library

A general library of full-length sessions

The library holds the full-length standard sessions of the curriculum. It opens on a featured session and a “jump back in” row, then lists the rest — aligned to their track so the shelf reads like the program.

  • Browse all, in one place. Full-length sessions across AI Officer, AI Engineering, and Leadership, side by side.
  • Featured head. A session from the learner's own track, with details and resume.
  • Jump back in. In-progress sessions surface first, so returning is one click.
  • Thumbnails and duration on every card, with a viewing-progress bar.

Filtering

Find the right session fast

As the catalogue grows, learners narrow the library by tag. Filters combine — pick one or many within a group, and stack groups together — with live counts and instant results.

  • Track — AI Officer, AI Engineering, Leadership.
  • Office — Revenue, Talent, Operations, Innovation.
  • Tools — Claude, ChatGPT, and more as the catalogue grows.
  • Length and Status — by duration, and by not started / in progress / watched.

Each option shows how many sessions it would return, and options that would return nothing are dimmed. Search runs across the same library. Sort by recommended, length, or title.

The player

Watch, connect, and keep going

The player is the same for every session. It plays the full-length video, remembers where the learner stopped, and points onward.

  • Watch and resume. A progress bar remembers the learner's place across sessions.
  • Connected to. Each video links out to its Mission Overview, Core Missions, Micro Sessions, and Blogs.
  • Up next. Suggested sessions from the same track, with an autoplay option.

Watch time

Watch time is data, not credit

Video logs how much of each session a learner watches. That signal is for people, not for the certification meter.

How watch time works

Watch time is logged as engagement data for coaches and program managers — how far a learner has watched, and where they stopped. It does not move the certification percentage.

Where credit comes from

Certification credit is earned by completing the session's challenge in the AI Buddy, not by watch time. Completion counts Core and electives together against a fixed denominator, so watching alone never marks a learner done.

Tracks

One track at a time, the whole library open

A learner works toward one certification at a time. Their track is featured and earns their credit; the other tracks stay open to watch, clearly marked as outside their certification.

The three tracks
AI Officer Enrolled AI Engineering Leadership

Watching a session from another track is always allowed — the time is still logged for the coach — but it is not part of the learner's current certification.
