The micro-session, finalized

The plan for the elective unit of the AI Officer Institute. A micro-session is a small, on-demand lesson — one video, one textbook, one short challenge — built from the same screens as a standard session. Complete the challenge in the AI Buddy and you earn one elective toward the certificate you are working on. This page is the settled shape: what it is, the flow, and the details.

What it is

A small elective, on the screens learners already know

A micro-session is exactly like a standard session, only smaller — so nothing new has to be learned to use it. It is on-demand first: a learner can start any time and work through it alone. A live sitting is an optional add-on, not the default.

  • Content: one video, one textbook, and one short challenge.
  • Identical UI: the micro-session screens match a standard session — same player, same reading pane, same challenge and AI Buddy.
  • Earns one elective: completing it contributes one required elective toward the current certificate.
  • Optional live sitting: the same session can also run live — sign up, get a Zoom link, join, and build a community around the topic.

The learner flow

Browse, do three things, earn the credit

One linear path, the same credit loop as every other unit: watch, read, do the challenge, submit. The challenge submission is the single act that earns the credit.

  1. Browse the catalog. Available (on-demand and upcoming live) and a past/completed archive; browse or filter by discipline.
  2. Open the session and watch the video. The full video plays; watch time counts toward progress.
  3. Read the textbook. One short reading scoped to the topic.
  4. Complete the challenge in the AI Buddy and submit. This is the act that earns credit — identical to a standard session.
  5. The elective is credited to the certificate the learner is currently working on, and it moves them one step closer to issuance.

Credit

One elective, applied once

Credit is earned by submitting the challenge, not by attending anything. Where that credit lands follows one clear rule.

Credit rule

A learner can apply any micro-session to the certification they are currently working on — like taking an elective in college (a Leadership-tagged session can count toward an AI Officer certification). It applies to that certification once; the same micro-session cannot be reused for a later certification. This is marked in the database. The Discipline tag is only a hint about which certificate a session most fits — it never limits what is eligible.

Tags

Two independent tag axes

Every micro-session carries two tags on two independent axes: which office it is about, and which discipline (certification) it most applies to. A session is one of each.

Office

What area of the business the session is about.

Revenue Talent Operations Innovation

Discipline

Which certification the session most applies to.

Leadership AI Engineering AI Officer

Worked example: a short “Prompt Ops” session reads as an Operations topic in the AI Officer discipline. More tag types will be added later as the catalog grows — for example tool tags such as Claude or ChatGPT.

Live option

An optional live sitting, with the states already defined

A micro-session can also be offered live. It never changes how credit is earned — the challenge submission still does that — it just adds a way to learn the topic together.

  • Sign up: a learner RSVPs, receives a Zoom link, and joins.
  • Community: the live sitting builds a community around the topic; the recording is archived afterward.
  • Button states: Start (on-demand default) — and when a live sitting exists, RSVP → View Session → View Recording.
  • Status: Upcoming · Missed · Completed for a live sitting.

Where it sits

One of three session types, one credit loop

Standard sessions, micro-sessions, and coaching differ in size and setting, not in how credit is earned. The micro-session is the small elective in the middle.

Standard session

The full-length session, the baseline unit of the curriculum. The Core missions are built from these.

Micro-session

The small elective: one video, one textbook, one short challenge. Identical screens to a standard session, plus an optional live sign-up.

Coaching

A live community session — bring a real AI challenge, get coached, and the recording is archived so others learn from it.

How it counts

Core plus Electives — no capstone

Micro-sessions are the elective half of a certification. Completion counts the Core missions and the required electives together, and the credential issues automatically when both are done. There is no capstone.

The rule that must hold

Completion percentage must count Core and Electives together, and the completion denominator must stay fixed at all the Core courses. When the Generative group was hidden as “coming soon”, the denominator shrank from 10 to 6, so students who finished only the 6 Agentic courses were shown as done and no credential ever issued (incident BE #942). Hiding content is presentational only — it must never change what “finished” means.

To decide

The few things still to lock

Everything above is settled behaviour or a known build. These are the open decisions to confirm before implementation.

  • Required elective count, per track. A target of 10 required micro-sessions is proposed but not settled — and it cannot be uniform: the AI Engineer Core is only 8 courses (E01–E08), so the rule needs to be defined per certification, not one number for all.
  • Two-axis tags need building. Office and Discipline do not exist in code yet; confirm the values (Office = Revenue / Talent / Operations / Innovation; Discipline = Leadership / AI Engineering / AI Officer) and add them.
  • Wire electives into completion. Extend the certificate's completion to count Core + Electives together, with the denominator fixed.
