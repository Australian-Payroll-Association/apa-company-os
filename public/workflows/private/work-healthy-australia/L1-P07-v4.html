Process 07: Assess and Control Workplace Risk

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
A Site, Department or Role to assess, and published Role requirements from Process 03 to assess against.
Trigger
An Employer requests an assessment, or the review date falls due and Process 08 raises it.
Output
Findings and a control plan for the Site, Department or Role. Never per Patient.
Output
A review date, which Process 08 turns into the next assessment.
Rule
The subject is the work, not the people in it. Surveying a group is one way to find a hazard, alongside observing the work being performed, measuring exposure, and reading the incident record.
Rule
A hazard enters the plan only if it could make someone unfit to perform their Role. That is what the assessment reads the Role requirements for, and it is the join between this process and 05b.
Rule
No identity gate and no consent gate, because there is no individual subject. Where a method does collect responses from individuals, the release gate is whether the finding can be published without identifying anyone, since a small enough group identifies its members by arithmetic.
Rule
Work Healthy Australia identifies, assesses and advises. Implementing the controls is the Employer's duty and cannot be handed to the provider. Safe Work Australia and ISO 45003 both put it there.
Rule
A person who wants something for themselves self-refers, which raises a Service Order and enters Process 05 with its own identity and consent gates. This process never refers anyone, because it never identifies anyone.
Rule
Each hazard carries a rating, not a yes or no. A rating is what lets a control plan be ordered, and what lets residual risk be recorded once a control is in place. The decision on the diagram is only whether the hazard reaches fitness at all.
Rule
Implementing controls is the Employer's duty. Recording which controls were implemented and when is not, and without it the review date has nothing to review.
Rule
The incident record is one of the four ways a hazard is found, and it is written at the Site when the incident happens. A recorded incident is itself a trigger for this process.
Rule
The findings go back to the people who answered before any action planning. Under the published method that is a consultation duty, not a courtesy. The review date carries the re-survey.
Open
Anonymous against confidential for the survey method. Anonymous means the respondent gets no personal result, so nothing triggers a self-referral. Confidential means a personal result to the worker alone and a self-request route. Ruled anonymous; the consequence is recorded and not resolved.
MVP scope
Surveys only, as the identification method. Observation, exposure measurement and incident review are the same spine with a different method in the middle, and are not in the first slice.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers
