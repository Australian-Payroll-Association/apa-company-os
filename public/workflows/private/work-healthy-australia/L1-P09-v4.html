Process 09: Report to Invoice

Prerequisite
Services rendered at a Site visit.
Trigger
A site visit ends. Reports each have their own cadence. The invoicing date depends on the client acceptance cycle.
Output
A report of the services rendered, against what was contracted.
Output
Clear invoices to be paid, built from the locked minutes.
Rule
Billing is time entry. There is no billable event. A Service Order is evidence that time was spent, never the thing that is charged.
Rule
The debrief is the time entry. It splits the day into travel, on site, debrief and internal minutes, and says which of them the client agreement covers.
Rule
A day is locked before it is invoiced, and after that the time records do not change. Unlocking is a named act by someone senior enough to do it, not an edit.
Rule
The Clinician or Practitioner completes and submits the debrief for their own site visit. Submission is never a precondition for invoicing.
Rule
Two chains that do not touch. An outstanding debrief holds the report of services rendered and nothing else.
Rule
A late cancellation and a failure to attend are recorded, and neither is a billing event. The time was reserved and consumed, so it is already in the minutes.
Rule
The report compares what was delivered against what was contracted, in minutes. That comparison is what a client renewing a contract asks for.
Roles
Clinicians, Practitioners, Admin, Employers
