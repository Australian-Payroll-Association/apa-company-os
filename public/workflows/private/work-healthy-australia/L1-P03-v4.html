Process 03: Define the Role Requirements

Prerequisite
Sites and Roles exist in the hierarchy.
Trigger
An Employer commissions the work, or the work a Role covers changes, or the review date falls due.
Output
A signed Role requirements document: the measured demands of the work. Every determination in Process 05b is made against it.
Output
The monitoring intervals that Process 08 reads.
Rule
The demands are measured on site by watching the work being performed. A job description is not evidence of what a job requires.
Rule
The document is the Employer's to sign. Until it is signed it is a draft and nothing may be determined against it.
Rule
The demands are recorded as measured values on the same scale the functional tests report, so Process 05b can compare the two. A document written as prose cannot be measured against.
Rule
The evidence is attached to the document: photographs, video, and the employer's own procedures. A signature on evidence nobody can see is not a yardstick.
Rule
Each task carries its risk rating, because the measurement that produces the demand also produces the risk. Process 07 reads these ratings and works at the Site and group level, above the single task.
The instruments
Which instrument is used is a choice below this level, and the step does not change when it changes. What the current estate already runs: ManTRA across five body regions, scoring time, duration, cycle, repetition, force, speed, exertion, awkwardness, vibration and cumulative risk; a risk matrix of probability, exposure and consequence; an overall demand rating from sedentary to very heavy; and minimum grip, pinch, shoulder push, shoulder pull and back ratings that the functional tests are compared against. Published alongside them: JobFit's PEFA sequence, REBA for whole-body posture, and the NIOSH equation for lifting. All produce a number on a scale, which is all this process requires of them.
Rule
A published document is never edited. A change publishes a new version and links back to the one it replaces. Work already booked completes against the version it started under, and new Service Orders pick up the new one.
