Process 05c: Provide A Service, review and release

Prerequisite
A determination recorded in 05b, not yet released.
Trigger
The determination is complete.
Output
A released determination, with the review and the reviewer recorded where review was required.
Output
A referral to the Patient's own doctor where something was found outside the referral question. The Patient is told of the finding.
Rule
Whether an output needs review before release is decided by the service, not by the person. Where it is required, the reviewer must hold the registration the service calls for, and who reviewed is recorded, not just that review happened.
Rule
Work Healthy Australia identifies and advises. The Employer decides placement.
What release means
Release is one act with no branch in it, which is why it has no subprocess. Four things happen and none of them is a decision. The determination and the plan are assembled into the Employer's copy, which carries the outcome, any restrictions, whether it is temporary, and the review date. Everything else is left behind: the history, the examination, the test results, the notes. It goes to the client contacts named in Process 01 as the ones who receive outputs, not to whoever raised the Order. And the sending is recorded, because an alert is not evidence of delivery. Where a determination is later amended, the amended copy replaces the first and says that it does.
Rule
The Patient is not sent the determination. The Employer commissioned the evaluation and receives it, and the Patient's route is the review after release, not a copy before it. This is the Australian commissioning model and it is the opposite of the British one, where the worker may read the report first, request corrections and withhold release. It is a decision, not a fact.
Rule
A clinically significant finding outside the referral question is a different thing from the determination, and the Patient is always told of it and referred to their own doctor. That is a duty of care and it does not cross the wall, because the Employer asked a different question.
Open
Whether a health check is treated the same way. A mini medical or an ergonomic assessment is a wellness service, not a fitness determination, and its result is arguably the Patient's rather than the Employer's. IMS already carries a patient opt-in on exactly those two and on nothing else. Not ruled.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers
