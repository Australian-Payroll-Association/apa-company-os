Process 01: Setup Workplace

Trigger
An agreement is signed.
Output
A new client is setup with their known hierarchy: Client organization > Site > Department > Role. A Department is optional. A Site may have none.
Output
The working days for the year on the Site and on each Department, and the working hours within those dates.
Output
The contracted services and the term they run for.
Output
The named client contacts, each with what they receive.
Output
Requirement defaults on the Site and the Role, which every later Service Order resolves against without a case-by-case decision.
Rule
A Client organization may sit under any number of parents, to any depth, because national and global umbrellas stack. Only the Australian Sites are in scope, and everything the processes need hangs off them.
Rule
The contracted services say what a client is entitled to. They carry no rates, because billing is time entry and not a charge per service.
Rule
Headcount sits on the Department and the Role, and so does whether workers rotate through tasks. Where they rotate, the demands that apply to a person are the union across the rotation, which is what Process 03 measures and Process 08 computes from.
Rule
Nothing is deleted. A Site that closes, a Department that merges or a Role that retires is end-dated, so every historical record stays resolvable and drops out of the pickers.
