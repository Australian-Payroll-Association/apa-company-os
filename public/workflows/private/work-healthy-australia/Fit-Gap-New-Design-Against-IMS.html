Fit and gap: the process set against IMS

Rewritten 2 August 2026. The earlier version listed forty-three gaps at mixed altitudes, most of which were either already settled or answerable from published practice. This version keeps only what is genuinely unresolved. It contains no discovery questions, because asking a client what their business does, after weeks on their systems, is how you lose them.

1. How this was filtered

Every item from the first pass was put through four questions in order.

  1. Has this already been decided? Then it is not a gap. It is a decision, and it is recorded in section 2.
  2. Can it be answered from published industry practice? Then we answer it and build it. Asking Work Healthy Australia something we could have read is worse than not asking. Section 3.
  3. Is it a design choice that belongs to us? Then it stays internal and does not go to the client. Section 4.
  4. Is it already answerable from evidence we hold? We hold three database schemas, a scraped screen reference, the reverse-engineering suite and the client's own feedback. If the answer is in there, we go and find it rather than ask. Section 6a.
  5. Is it a commercial choice that is genuinely theirs? Then it goes to them as a proposal with a default, never as an open question. Section 6b.

Forty-three items went in. Nothing comes out as a question. Five became findings we could establish from evidence already in hand, and four became proposals with a default already chosen.

3. Settled by published practice. We build it, we do not ask.

Each of these was raised as a gap. Each has a published answer, so it is now a build requirement in version 4 rather than a question. The source is named so the decision is auditable.

WhatThe answer, and where it comes from
Does a determination need review before release?Yes, and the tier depends on the service. OSHGroup publishes allied health delivering, a registrar reviewing, and escalation to an occupational physician for sign-off. KINNECT signs off every result with a doctor for uniformity of opinion. Rail puts Authorised Health Professionals under a Chief Medical Officer. Queensland coal routes the form from examining doctor to Appointed Medical Adviser
Is consent to hold records the same as consent to a procedure?No. They are separate, and the second is taken before the procedure. Standard clinical practice, and consistent with the workflow feedback received in July
Should demands and abilities be on one scale?Yes. JobFit's decision rule is that capability equals or exceeds the documented demand, compared on the same scale. A prose document cannot be measured against
Is a job description enough evidence?No. AFOEM is explicit that requirements are only objectively assessed by worksite visits. Photographs, video and the employer's own procedures are standard job task analysis practice
What happens to work in flight when a role document changes?It completes against the version it started under. New orders pick up the new version. Normative FHIR guidance and standard master-data practice
Should hazards be rated or decided yes or no?Rated. Consequence, exposure and probability is the Safe Work Australia model, and it is the only way to order a control plan or record residual risk
Who implements controls, and does anyone check?The Employer implements, and that duty cannot be delegated. Recording whether it happened is a different act and belongs to the provider, or the review date reviews nothing
Does a stalled return-to-work case need a reason recorded?Yes. Biopsychosocial barriers, identified and separately resolved, are standard return-to-work practice
Should a person be told a finding outside the referral question?Yes, and referred to their own doctor. AFOEM duty of care. It does not cross the wall
Do two clinicians perform the same services?No. Registration type and scope of practice decide what a person may perform, separately from what data they may see
Should time records be locked before invoicing?Yes. Basic financial control. An invoice built from mutable records is not evidence
Do travel time, headcount, rotation, public holidays and named contacts matter?Yes, all of them, and all are ordinary. A mobile workforce needs travel in the slot search, rotation means a person's demands are the union across the tasks they rotate through, and a report needs an addressee

None of these needed a question. All are in version 4.

6. What we established, and the four things we ask them to confirm

There are no discovery questions here. Asking a client what their business does, after weeks on their systems, is the fastest way to lose them. Everything below either states a finding with its source, or puts a proposal in front of them with a default already chosen.

6a. Established from evidence we already hold. Not asked.

The first pass produced questions for Work Healthy Australia that our own material already answers. They are answered here instead.

Question we were going to askThe answer, and how we know
Do you still deliver treatment?Yes, and it is the largest volume. The scraped screen reference records complaint types EI, HC, MCP and Rehab, and notes that Early Intervention is the most common type at Work Healthy Australia. The treatment chain is fully built: interview, examination, diagnosis, treatment summary, review and post-treatment screens, with a treatment plan expressed as sessions per week for a total number of visits, and a modality list covering soft tissue work, manipulation, dry needling and exercise rehabilitation
Do you record incidents and first aid?Yes. The Visit Summary carries an Injury and Illness Report section alongside Complaints, PES, Consultations, Work Hardening, Mini Health Examination, FFT, Ergonomic Assessments and RTW Programs. The first aid tables and their lookups for nature, mechanism, location and agency are all present
Do you record drug and alcohol results?Yes, inside pre-employment screening. The result tables exist and the PES screen includes it as a sub-test. This does not disturb the standing rule that Work Healthy Australia does no sampling. Recording a result somebody else collected is a different act from collecting it, and the scope rule stands unchanged
Do you run workforce surveys?No, and that is the opportunity rather than a gap. What exists today is per-patient validated outcome questionnaires, which are clinical measures inside a treatment episode. The workforce survey capability is named after a survey product that no longer exists. Australia's only government-validated psychosocial survey is being decommissioned on 2 October 2026, which is the opening
Who maintains the client hierarchy?Work Healthy Australia does. There are no administrators, and clinicians do everything, so custodianship is provider-mediated by necessity rather than by choice. The design assumes this

That leaves nothing to ask about how the business works.

7. What row counts would add, and why they are not blocking

A single read-only query over the live database would return the row count of every table. Matched against section 5, it upgrades each Covered from *IMS can hold this* to *IMS holds this and still writes to it*.

That changes migration effort. It does not change the design. The flows were drawn from the business and tested against published practice, and IMS was the third pass, whose only job was to label each step migrate or build. If every table came back empty, not one box on any diagram would move.

The specific claims a row count would most usefully test, recorded now so the prediction is on the record rather than adjusted afterwards: whether the release-approval flag on the eight assessment types is actually set, whether the return-to-work barrier tables carry rows, and whether the workforce survey tables are live or a tombstone.

None of that blocks anything, and none of it is worth a client conversation.

8. Re-placed at L2, and what that changed

Added 2 August, after the subprocesses were drawn. The earlier mapping put IMS tables against L1 boxes. Most of those tables are L2-shaped, so the comparison was made at the wrong altitude, and that is what manufactured gaps out of things that were only ever detail. With eight subprocesses drawn, the tables now sit against the steps they actually belong to.

The result is not a longer list. It is a shorter and sharper one, and it moves in both directions.

What this says about the method

The altitude error was real and it cost a round. Mapping a schema to a process only works when both are at the same level, and a legacy schema sits far lower than an L1 diagram. The rule to carry forward is that a table is compared to a step, never to a process, and if there is no step at that granularity yet then the comparison has to wait for one.

It also means the honest sequence is design first, decompose where the design demands it, and only then place the legacy. Placing it earlier is what produces a list of forty-three things that reads as urgent and is mostly noise.
