Edge8 · Work Healthy Australia · 3 August 2026 · Authorization is answerable by a query, and the wall is a schema grant. Both are claims a verifier can test without reading the code. A Patient reaches their own record two ways: an applicant through a scoped, expiring link with no session, an employee by signing in. Every other policy in the product is scoped to a Client organization; a Patient’s is scoped to their own person record, because an employee must not see the colleague sitting next to them. An employee’s account is provisioned from their first Service Order, not from an onboarding step, because there is no worker roster.
