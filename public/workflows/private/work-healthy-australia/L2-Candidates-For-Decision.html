Which L1 steps need an L2 drawn under them

A working list, 2 August 2026. Every box in the eleven diagrams was tested against one question, and the answer decides whether it gets a subprocess.

The test

A box needs an L2 when a builder could not build it from the box alone, because underneath it sits a sequence with its own decisions, its own failure modes, or its own actors.

A box does not need an L2 when what sits underneath is a list of fields, a choice of instrument, or a single write. That is L3, and drawing it as process is the trap.

Applied to every box, twenty-three come out as candidates. Nine are worth drawing. The rest are recorded here so the judgement is on the record rather than repeated.

That last one is the only genuine omission in the set rather than a decomposition. Everything else on this page exists as a box already.

Recommendation

Draw one to five. They are four cash-or-exposure items and the largest build on the list, and each is a real algorithm that a builder cannot infer.

Hold six to nine until one to five are drawn, then reassess, because two of them may collapse into the ones above once those exist.

Leave the rest. The list stays here so nothing is lost, and nothing on it is blocking.
