Process 08: Track Fitness Check-ups

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
Requirements and intervals on the Site and the Role from Processes 01 and 03. Every other date is set by the process that made the judgement: credential expiries from 02, review dates on temporary determinations from 05b, control plan review dates from 07, and certificate expiries from 06.
Trigger
A date. Nothing in this process waits for a person to ask.
Output
A Service Order for each due item, raised without an Employer request and scheduled through Process 04.
Output
A due and overdue position per Site, crossing the wall as dates and counts with no clinical detail.
Rule
The requirement lives on the Site and the Role, never on the person. There is no worker roster, because a person is not onboarded as an employee, so the Role comes in on the Service Order from the Employer who nominated them. What is due is computed from that Role and that Site, and it changes when the Employer nominates against a different one.
Rule
Two things set an interval and both are read. A test carries its own validity period, which is a property of the test. A Site or a Role may require it sooner. The shorter of the two wins, and neither can extend the other.
Open
Whether credential expiries belong here. Process 08 reads them today, but a First Aid ticket is not a fitness check-up. Either they move, or this process is named slightly narrower than its contents. Not ruled.
Rule
This is the only process in the set that watches a calendar. Nothing else waits on a date. Other processes decide what the date should be and hand it here, which is why 06 and 08 both end by setting one.
Rule
It raises the Service Order and stops. Process 04 schedules it and Process 05 performs it. Nothing is rendered here.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Employers
