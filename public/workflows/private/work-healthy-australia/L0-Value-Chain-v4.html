L0 Value Chain, version 4

Nine processes in five bands. A band says what kind of work it is: set up once per client, raise the work, schedule it, deliver it, settle it. Every arrow carries its own words.

01 to 05 and 09 are the core flow, the chain that takes a client from a signed agreement to an invoice. 06, 07 and 08 are potential processes, drawn with a dashed border. They are not what Work Healthy Australia does today. They are here because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found all three in every mature provider, and because the core flow cannot deliver them as it stands.

The processes

Process 01: Setup Workplace

Trigger
An agreement is signed. Later, a Client, Site or Department is added or retired.
Output
The client hierarchy: Organization > Site > Department > Role.
Output
A Site Service calendar carrying the Site's operating hours.
Output
Requirement defaults on each Site and Role, so a later request resolves what is needed without a case-by-case decision.
Roles
Admin

Process 02: Onboard / Offboard

Prerequisite
A valid Organization and Site.
Trigger
A person joins or retires. A credential reaches its expiry.
Output
Clinicians, Practitioners and Employers assigned to the right Site, with authorization scoped, and on the Site Service calendar.
Output
Credentials recorded with their expiry, which Process 08 reads.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 03: Define the Role Requirements

Prerequisite
Sites and Roles exist.
Trigger
A Role is created, or the work it covers changes, or the review date falls due.
Output
A signed Role requirements document: the measured demands of the work, which every determination is made against.
Output
The monitoring intervals that Process 08 reads.
Roles
Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 04: Schedule the Service

Prerequisite
A staffed Site Service calendar. Every Service Order carries an estimated period.
Trigger
A Service Order is raised by an Employer, by Process 08, or by a Case. A booked Patient does not attend. A Clinician or Practitioner becomes unavailable.
Output
An appointment on the Site Service calendar, booked as a placeholder, with the paperwork issued before the day.
Output
A weekly no-show report.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Admin, Employers

Process 05: Provide A Service

Prerequisite
A Service Order, and published Role requirements to measure against.
Trigger
A job applicant or an employee attends.
Output
An evaluation, fit or fit with restrictions or not fit, with a review date where it is temporary, delivered to the Employer with a mitigation plan. Per Patient.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 06: Manage the Unfit CasePotential

Prerequisite
A determination of not fit, or fit with restrictions to manage.
Trigger
A case is opened. Then every determination that comes back from a re-assessment Process 08 raised.
Output
A suitable duties plan revised against each certificate, and a closed case once capacity is durable.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 07: Assess and Control Workplace RiskPotential

Prerequisite
A Site, Department or Role to assess, and published Role requirements to assess against.
Trigger
An Employer requests an assessment, or the review date falls due.
Output
Findings and a control plan for the Site, Department or Role, never per Patient. The subject is the work, not the people in it, so there is no identity gate and no consent gate.
Output
A review date, which Process 08 turns into the next assessment.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 08: Track Fitness Check-upsPotential

Prerequisite
Requirements and intervals on the Site and the Role.
Trigger
A date. Nothing here waits for a person to ask, and nothing else in the set watches a calendar.
Output
Service Orders raised on their due date, and a due and overdue position per Site that the Employer can see.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Employers

Process 09: Report to Invoice

Prerequisite
Services rendered at a Site visit.
Trigger
A site visit ends. The client invoice cycle begins.
Output
A report of the services rendered.
Output
Clear invoices to be paid.
Roles
Clinicians, Practitioners, Admin, Employers
