Process 01: Setup Workplace

Trigger
An agreement is signed.
Output
A new client is setup with their known hierarchy: Client organization > Site > Department > Role. A Department is optional. A Site may have none.
Output
The working days for the year on the Site and on each Department, and the working hours within those dates.
Output
The contracted services and the term they run for.
Output
The named client contacts, each with what they receive.
Output
Requirement defaults on the Site and the Role, which every later Service Order resolves against without a case-by-case decision.
Rule
A Client organization may sit under any number of parents, to any depth, because national and global umbrellas stack. Only the Australian Sites are in scope, and everything the processes need hangs off them.
Rule
The contracted services say what a client is entitled to. They carry no rates, because billing is time entry and not a charge per service.
Rule
Headcount sits on the Department and the Role, and so does whether workers rotate through tasks. Where they rotate, the demands that apply to a person are the union across the rotation, which is what Process 03 measures and Process 08 computes from.
Rule
Nothing is deleted. A Site that closes, a Department that merges or a Role that retires is end-dated, so every historical record stays resolvable and drops out of the pickers.
Roles
Admin

Process 02: Onboard / Offboard Clinician, Practitioner, and Employer

Prerequisite
A valid Organization > Site must be existing. A Department is an optional attribute.
Trigger
A new person joins or an existing person retires.
Output
Practitioners and Employers are assigned to the correct Site, or Department where applicable.
Output
Site Service calendar has Practitioner and Clinician assigned so services can be rendered.
Output
On offboard, the person is removed from the Site or Department and from the Site Service calendar.
Output
A grant of the services each person may perform, derived from the credentials just confirmed.
Rule
Authorization has two halves. Data scope says which records a person may see, and it is what the five roles carry. Function scope says which services a person may perform, and it comes from their credentials. Two Clinicians are not interchangeable, because one may be credentialled for a service the other is not.
Rule
A credential carries its type, the state it was issued in, and its expiry. The expiry is a date, so Process 08 reads it.
Rule
Every grant and every revocation is dated, and access is archived rather than deleted, so a past action can be attributed to the scope that was in force when it happened.
Rule
The Patient is never onboarded here. A person applies under a personal email address and receives employee services under a company email address, and those stay two separate records. The Employer decides which employees need a service, so there is no worker roster to maintain.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 03: Define the Role Requirements

Prerequisite
Sites and Roles exist in the hierarchy.
Trigger
An Employer commissions the work, or the work a Role covers changes, or the review date falls due.
Output
A signed Role requirements document: the measured demands of the work. Every determination in Process 05b is made against it.
Output
The monitoring intervals that Process 08 reads.
Rule
The demands are measured on site by watching the work being performed. A job description is not evidence of what a job requires.
Rule
The document is the Employer's to sign. Until it is signed it is a draft and nothing may be determined against it.
Rule
The demands are recorded as measured values on the same scale the functional tests report, so Process 05b can compare the two. A document written as prose cannot be measured against.
Rule
The evidence is attached to the document: photographs, video, and the employer's own procedures. A signature on evidence nobody can see is not a yardstick.
Rule
Each task carries its risk rating, because the measurement that produces the demand also produces the risk. Process 07 reads these ratings and works at the Site and group level, above the single task.
The instruments
Which instrument is used is a choice below this level, and the step does not change when it changes. What the current estate already runs: ManTRA across five body regions, scoring time, duration, cycle, repetition, force, speed, exertion, awkwardness, vibration and cumulative risk; a risk matrix of probability, exposure and consequence; an overall demand rating from sedentary to very heavy; and minimum grip, pinch, shoulder push, shoulder pull and back ratings that the functional tests are compared against. Published alongside them: JobFit's PEFA sequence, REBA for whole-body posture, and the NIOSH equation for lifting. All produce a number on a scale, which is all this process requires of them.
Rule
A published document is never edited. A change publishes a new version and links back to the one it replaces. Work already booked completes against the version it started under, and new Service Orders pick up the new one.
Roles
Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 04: Schedule the Service

Prerequisite
The Site Service calendar exists, carries the Site's operating hours, and has a Clinician or Practitioner assigned to it. Every Service Order carries an estimated period.
Trigger
A Service Order is raised for a Site, a booked Patient does not attend, or a Clinician or Practitioner becomes unavailable for a date range.
Urgent
A complaint or injury Order may be marked urgent by the Employer raising it, and only that kind may be. Urgent means someone is hurt or a situation is unsafe now, so the Order does not join the queue. It lands as an alert on the Day Sheet of the Clinician or Practitioner covering the Site the moment it is raised, and is seen today. An emergency is a different thing again: an ambulance and the emergency department, not an Order, which is the triage line every industry reference draws.
Rule
The Day Sheet is the day's booked sessions for one Clinician or Practitioner at one Site, read from the Site Service calendar. The urgent alert lands there because the person who owns the day is the only one who can rule how the day absorbs the case. Drawn for the Clinician; a Practitioner rules their own Day Sheet the same way inside their own Client organization.
Rule
Two ways to absorb it, and the day owner chooses. Push the day: every remaining session moves back one, and the day runs past the Site's hours into overtime where it must. Clear the day: every non-critical service on the sheet is rebooked to the next available slot through the normal search, and the freed time takes the case. What counts as critical is decided by the service, not by the person: another injury does not move, a screening does. Every moved Patient and their Employer contact are told at the moment of the move, the same way any booking is confirmed. Overtime needs no special object, because Process 09 bills time and a long day is simply more recorded time.
Open
Whether an Order raised by a case in Process 06 or by the clock in Process 08 may also be urgent. Today only the Employer marks it, at raise time, on a complaint or injury Order. Not ruled.
Output
An appointment on the Site Service calendar: a named Clinician or Practitioner, a Patient, and a slot of the estimated period inside the Site's operating hours. Booked as a placeholder until accepted.
Output
Where there is no capacity in the Site's hours, the Order is returned unbooked. Site, date and time cross the wall. Nothing else.
Output
A weekly no-show report of the Patients who did not attend, carrying the open Service Orders that need follow-up.
Rule
An open Service Order is never dropped, and it is never rebooked by default. Where Work Healthy Australia moves the date, the Order keeps a slot. Where the Patient does not attend, the Order releases its slot and waits on the no-show report until follow-up books it again.
Rule
The schedule is a plan, not a gate. It never decides how released time is used and never prevents a Patient being seen earlier than the slotted time.
Rule
A Service Order raised by the clock in Process 08, or by a case in Process 06, enters at the search with no Employer behind it. An Employer amending a booking they already hold enters the same way.
Rule
The slot search reads travel time as well as the Site's working hours. A slot the assigned person cannot physically reach is not a slot.
Rule
The estimated period comes from the client and the service together, not from the service alone, because the same service runs longer at some Sites. Where that resolution happens is below this level.
Rule
The Patient is entitled to be served under this Client organization when their work email address belongs to it. The Service Order carries that check, so the door in 05a verifies who a person is rather than whether they belong.
Rule
Confirmation goes to both the named Employer contact and the Patient at the moment of booking, and the paperwork goes out before the day.
Rule
The Order carries a short check-in code from the moment it is raised, the way an airline issues a booking reference, so a walk-in with no booking still arrives holding one. It is a correlation key, not a credential. One code ties the Service Order, the appointment, the paperwork issued before the day, the consents given against it, and later the outputs, so any one of them pulls up all of them with certainty. A booking reference ties a reservation to checked luggage, a boarding pass and a screening status, and it still does not board anyone. Identity is a separate act, in Process 05a.
Rule
An Employer request that cannot be met is returned with a reason, the same way the no-slot alert is. A request falling outside the contracted working days is a different case from one that fits, and is returned as such.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Admin, Employers

Process 05a: Provide A Service, admission: identity and consent

Prerequisite
A Service Order exists for the Patient. An appointment from Process 04 is the normal path to one, and is not a gate.
Trigger
A job applicant or an employee attends against a Service Order, booked or walked in.
Rule
The appointment is never checked at the door, the Service Order is. The schedule is a plan, so a booked Patient may arrive early and an unbooked one may walk in. Where no Order exists, because the Employer sent the person down with nothing raised, the named Employer contact raises one themselves while the person waits: they name the person in the system, raise the Order, and text the check-in code the Order issues, so the walk down the corridor and the code arriving by text are one act. Raising an Order is the Employer's act and never Work Healthy Australia's. Nobody is served without one, and an attempt that ends without an Order leaves no trace on any Patient record.
Rule
The Order is the Employer's record and Work Healthy Australia never edits it. One raised against the wrong Site or Department is returned to the named Employer contact to amend, and the service proceeds once it is amended, the same visit where the day allows. The Site matters because the requirement defaults, the calendar and the billing all resolve from it, so a service rendered against the wrong Site is measured against the wrong yardstick.
Rule
Employment is never verified, because no client hands over their HR system and no provider in the industry set asks for it. The Service Order is the Employer's attestation that the person is their current employee or their applicant for the Role, made when the service was commissioned, and keeping it current is the Employer's duty. The work email address corroborates an employee. An applicant is whoever the referral names.
Gate 1
Identity. No record is opened, read or amended until the person attending is confirmed to be the Patient named on the Service Order. A mismatch stops the service and leaves no trace on any record.
Rule
Locating and identifying are two acts and the check-in code only does the first. The code pulls up the Service Order and everything tied to it with certainty, which removes the search and the mis-selection. It proves nothing about who is holding it, so identity is still confirmed in the room against government-issued identification. A code can no more replace a passport than a booking reference can.
The record
A Patient record scoped to this Client organization is held before any consent or clinical content is captured, because both are written into it. It is a step rather than a gate, because once identity is confirmed a record can always be held: an exact match within this Client is confirmed with the person and reused, and no match creates one. An applicant's records under other Client organizations are not this record. They are never read, never matched against, never copied from, and all history and every consent are captured fresh for this Client.
Rule
The duplicate search runs within this Client organization only, on the name and date of birth as they appear on the identification already in hand from Gate 1, so the identity check and the duplicate check are one act on one document. A partial match is a decision for the Clinician in the room, never a merge the system performs on its own. No search ever crosses Clients, because nothing may be reused across them and the search itself would be a use of another Client's records.
Rule
The identification is sighted and the sighting recorded: the kind of document and that it matched. The document number is not retained, because holding government identifiers is restricted under the privacy principles and the number buys nothing once the sighting is on the record.
Gate 2
Consent. Consent for Work Healthy Australia to hold the Patient's records is given once per Client organization. On every later visit under that Client it is checked, not asked again. The same Patient under a different Client consents again. Anything still outstanding for this Service Order is captured before any clinical work.
Output
An admitted Patient with every required consent recorded, ready for the service to be rendered in 05b.
Output
Or the service stops. The Employer is told it was not completed, with no reason and no clinical detail, whichever gate stopped it.
Rule
Consent is collected before attendance with the paperwork Process 04 issues, so the door verifies rather than collects, and only captures what is still outstanding. Gate 1 stays at the door because identity cannot be confirmed remotely.
Rule
Consent is a set, not one thing, and each is held against the version of the wording that was signed. When the wording changes, consent held against the old version is no longer current and is asked again.
The set
Eight, from KINNECT's published consent guide, Sonic HealthPlus's privacy and consent form, AFOEM guidance, and what the current estate already stores. 1. Take part in the assessment at all. 2. Collect and hold personal and health information. 3. Release the outcome to the Employer who commissioned it. 4. Contact the Patient's own treating doctor, which every source keeps separate and in writing. 5. Drug and alcohol testing where the service includes it. 6. Receive survey invitations electronically. 7. Use de-identified data to improve the service. 8. Use data in research, which is explicit and separate from the seventh. Informed consent to a procedure is a ninth and is taken in 05b, not here, because it is consent to an act rather than to holding a record.
Rule
Refusing is a legitimate recorded outcome, not a failure. KINNECT states it plainly: without consent there is no report. What follows is that the service does not proceed, and the Employer is told it was not completed with no reason attached.
Open
Whether consent held for one kind of service covers another. The rule today is once per Client organization for records consent. The narrower reading is once per Client organization per service type, on the ground that consenting to a complaint record is not consenting to a pre-employment screen. Not ruled.
Open
Whether hiring joins the records. Process 02 rules that a person's applicant record and their employee record stay two separate records even under the same Client organization. The industry pattern joins them at hire, because the pre-employment baseline feeds the surveillance that follows and 05b reviews a standing history at every visit. The candidate rule is that hire is the returning-person case: an explicit, consented, Clinician-mediated join at the first visit as an employee, never an automatic one. Not ruled.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Employers

Process 05b: Provide A Service, render and determine

Prerequisite
A Patient admitted through 05a. Identity confirmed, consents recorded.
Trigger
A potentially open Role that a Patient needs to fill, whether through pre-employment screening or because an employee is not fit for their current role.
Output
An evaluation in one of three states, fit, fit with restrictions, or not fit, delivered to the Employer, with a mitigation plan where restrictions apply or the person is not fit. Per Patient.
Output
A determination that is not released here. Release, and the review before it, are Process 05c.
Rule
A restriction is measured, never asserted. Anything other than plain fit routes through functional testing against the Role requirements before the restrictions are recorded.
Rule
A safety gate sits before functional testing. Where an existing injury is disclosed, clearance comes first, because a lifting assessment can injure someone.
Rule
The determination records whether it is temporary or permanent, and a temporary one carries a review date. That date is what Process 08 reads.
Rule
The standing medical history is reviewed and updated at every visit. A longitudinal record nobody revisits is wrong within a year.
Rule
Consent to hold records, which Process 05a gates, is not consent to perform a procedure. Informed consent to the procedure is taken here, against what it involves, its risks and benefits, and the alternatives.
Rule
Drawn for the Clinician. A Practitioner renders the same way inside their own Client organization, and where the Site is outside it, no Patient or Service data is returned.
Rule
Documents belong to the record: the signed consent, an external certificate, an imaging report. Time spent is recorded against the service, because Process 09 bills time.
Rule
A standing alert on a Patient or a Client organization is shown when the record is opened. It is not an exception, it is a flag, and nothing clinical may be written into one, because it is read outside the wall.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 05c: Provide A Service, review and release

Prerequisite
A determination recorded in 05b, not yet released.
Trigger
The determination is complete.
Output
A released determination, with the review and the reviewer recorded where review was required.
Output
A referral to the Patient's own doctor where something was found outside the referral question. The Patient is told of the finding.
Rule
Whether an output needs review before release is decided by the service, not by the person. Where it is required, the reviewer must hold the registration the service calls for, and who reviewed is recorded, not just that review happened.
Rule
Work Healthy Australia identifies and advises. The Employer decides placement.
What release means
Release is one act with no branch in it, which is why it has no subprocess. Four things happen and none of them is a decision. The determination and the plan are assembled into the Employer's copy, which carries the outcome, any restrictions, whether it is temporary, and the review date. Everything else is left behind: the history, the examination, the test results, the notes. It goes to the client contacts named in Process 01 as the ones who receive outputs, not to whoever raised the Order. And the sending is recorded, because an alert is not evidence of delivery. Where a determination is later amended, the amended copy replaces the first and says that it does.
Rule
The Patient is not sent the determination. The Employer commissioned the evaluation and receives it, and the Patient's route is the review after release, not a copy before it. This is the Australian commissioning model and it is the opposite of the British one, where the worker may read the report first, request corrections and withhold release. It is a decision, not a fact.
Rule
A clinically significant finding outside the referral question is a different thing from the determination, and the Patient is always told of it and referred to their own doctor. That is a duty of care and it does not cross the wall, because the Employer asked a different question.
Open
Whether a health check is treated the same way. A mini medical or an ergonomic assessment is a wellness service, not a fitness determination, and its result is arguably the Patient's rather than the Employer's. IMS already carries a patient opt-in on exactly those two and on nothing else. Not ruled.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 06: Manage the Unfit Case

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
A determination of not fit, or fit with restrictions to manage, from Process 05b.
Trigger
The case is opened. Then every certificate expiry.
Output
A suitable duties plan, agreed by the Patient and the Employer and revised against each new certificate.
Output
A closed case once capacity is restored and durable.
Rule
The case owns no calendar. The certificate's expiry is a date, and Process 08 is the only thing that watches dates. The case sets the date, hands it over, and stops until a determination comes back.
Rule
An unfit case means a Patient who is not fit, or fit only with restrictions, for the Role as it stands. Most of the volume is the second kind.
Rule
What crosses the wall is duties and dates. The Employer needs to know what the person may do and until when, and nothing else.
Rule
This process holds the state and makes the judgements. It knows what one Patient may do right now and whether that has settled. Process 08 knows when something is due. That is the whole difference between them.
Rule
Drawn for the Clinician. A Practitioner manages cases the same way inside their own Client organization.
Rule
The case carries the claim reference where there is one, so external documents tie together. Work Healthy Australia does not manage the claim, and the insurer is not a party to this process.
Rule
Whether a suitable duties plan is possible at all is a separate question from how it is drafted, and it is asked first. Where the Site has no duties that fit the measured restrictions, there is no plan to agree, and saying so is a different answer from an Employer who agreed and then did not implement. Conflating the two hides the case that needs the most attention.
Rule
Agreeing the duties is not the same as putting them in place. The case checks that the agreed duties are actually being worked, and where they are not, that is raised with the Employer and recorded as a barrier.
Rule
Barriers to recovery are recorded, and separately marked resolved. A case that will not close has a reason, and it is almost always here.
Rule
Advice is split by who it is for. Advice to the Employer, advice about the work, and advice for the Patient at home are three different things, and only the first crosses the wall.
Rule
Treatment is called from here and tracked, and it is not defined here. Work Healthy Australia refers, records attendance and progress, and re-assesses. What happens in a treatment session sits below this level, whoever delivers it.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 07: Assess and Control Workplace Risk

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
A Site, Department or Role to assess, and published Role requirements from Process 03 to assess against.
Trigger
An Employer requests an assessment, or the review date falls due and Process 08 raises it.
Output
Findings and a control plan for the Site, Department or Role. Never per Patient.
Output
A review date, which Process 08 turns into the next assessment.
Rule
The subject is the work, not the people in it. Surveying a group is one way to find a hazard, alongside observing the work being performed, measuring exposure, and reading the incident record.
Rule
A hazard enters the plan only if it could make someone unfit to perform their Role. That is what the assessment reads the Role requirements for, and it is the join between this process and 05b.
Rule
No identity gate and no consent gate, because there is no individual subject. Where a method does collect responses from individuals, the release gate is whether the finding can be published without identifying anyone, since a small enough group identifies its members by arithmetic.
Rule
Work Healthy Australia identifies, assesses and advises. Implementing the controls is the Employer's duty and cannot be handed to the provider. Safe Work Australia and ISO 45003 both put it there.
Rule
A person who wants something for themselves self-refers, which raises a Service Order and enters Process 05 with its own identity and consent gates. This process never refers anyone, because it never identifies anyone.
Rule
Each hazard carries a rating, not a yes or no. A rating is what lets a control plan be ordered, and what lets residual risk be recorded once a control is in place. The decision on the diagram is only whether the hazard reaches fitness at all.
Rule
Implementing controls is the Employer's duty. Recording which controls were implemented and when is not, and without it the review date has nothing to review.
Rule
The incident record is one of the four ways a hazard is found, and it is written at the Site when the incident happens. A recorded incident is itself a trigger for this process.
Rule
The findings go back to the people who answered before any action planning. Under the published method that is a consultation duty, not a courtesy. The review date carries the re-survey.
Open
Anonymous against confidential for the survey method. Anonymous means the respondent gets no personal result, so nothing triggers a self-referral. Confidential means a personal result to the worker alone and a self-request route. Ruled anonymous; the consequence is recorded and not resolved.
MVP scope
Surveys only, as the identification method. Observation, exposure measurement and incident review are the same spine with a different method in the middle, and are not in the first slice.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers

Process 08: Track Fitness Check-ups

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
Requirements and intervals on the Site and the Role from Processes 01 and 03. Every other date is set by the process that made the judgement: credential expiries from 02, review dates on temporary determinations from 05b, control plan review dates from 07, and certificate expiries from 06.
Trigger
A date. Nothing in this process waits for a person to ask.
Output
A Service Order for each due item, raised without an Employer request and scheduled through Process 04.
Output
A due and overdue position per Site, crossing the wall as dates and counts with no clinical detail.
Rule
The requirement lives on the Site and the Role, never on the person. There is no worker roster, because a person is not onboarded as an employee, so the Role comes in on the Service Order from the Employer who nominated them. What is due is computed from that Role and that Site, and it changes when the Employer nominates against a different one.
Rule
Two things set an interval and both are read. A test carries its own validity period, which is a property of the test. A Site or a Role may require it sooner. The shorter of the two wins, and neither can extend the other.
Open
Whether credential expiries belong here. Process 08 reads them today, but a First Aid ticket is not a fitness check-up. Either they move, or this process is named slightly narrower than its contents. Not ruled.
Rule
This is the only process in the set that watches a calendar. Nothing else waits on a date. Other processes decide what the date should be and hand it here, which is why 06 and 08 both end by setting one.
Rule
It raises the Service Order and stops. Process 04 schedules it and Process 05 performs it. Nothing is rendered here.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Employers

Process 09: Report to Invoice

Prerequisite
Services rendered at a Site visit.
Trigger
A site visit ends. Reports each have their own cadence. The invoicing date depends on the client acceptance cycle.
Output
A report of the services rendered, against what was contracted.
Output
Clear invoices to be paid, built from the locked minutes.
Rule
Billing is time entry. There is no billable event. A Service Order is evidence that time was spent, never the thing that is charged.
Rule
The debrief is the time entry. It splits the day into travel, on site, debrief and internal minutes, and says which of them the client agreement covers.
Rule
A day is locked before it is invoiced, and after that the time records do not change. Unlocking is a named act by someone senior enough to do it, not an edit.
Rule
The Clinician or Practitioner completes and submits the debrief for their own site visit. Submission is never a precondition for invoicing.
Rule
Two chains that do not touch. An outstanding debrief holds the report of services rendered and nothing else.
Rule
A late cancellation and a failure to attend are recorded, and neither is a billing event. The time was reserved and consumed, so it is already in the minutes.
Rule
The report compares what was delivered against what was contracted, in minutes. That comparison is what a client renewing a contract asks for.
Roles
Clinicians, Practitioners, Admin, Employers
