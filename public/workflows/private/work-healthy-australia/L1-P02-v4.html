Process 02: Onboard / Offboard Clinician, Practitioner, and Employer

Prerequisite
A valid Organization > Site must be existing. A Department is an optional attribute.
Trigger
A new person joins or an existing person retires.
Output
Practitioners and Employers are assigned to the correct Site, or Department where applicable.
Output
Site Service calendar has Practitioner and Clinician assigned so services can be rendered.
Output
On offboard, the person is removed from the Site or Department and from the Site Service calendar.
Output
A grant of the services each person may perform, derived from the credentials just confirmed.
Rule
Authorization has two halves. Data scope says which records a person may see, and it is what the five roles carry. Function scope says which services a person may perform, and it comes from their credentials. Two Clinicians are not interchangeable, because one may be credentialled for a service the other is not.
Rule
A credential carries its type, the state it was issued in, and its expiry. The expiry is a date, so Process 08 reads it.
Rule
Every grant and every revocation is dated, and access is archived rather than deleted, so a past action can be attributed to the scope that was in force when it happened.
Rule
The Patient is never onboarded here. A person applies under a personal email address and receives employee services under a company email address, and those stay two separate records. The Employer decides which employees need a service, so there is no worker roster to maintain.
Roles
Admin, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers
