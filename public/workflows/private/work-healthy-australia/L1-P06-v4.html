Process 06: Manage the Unfit Case

Status
Potential process. It is not what Work Healthy Australia does today. It is drawn because the sweep of KINNECT, Jobfit, Sonic HealthPlus, the Netherlands, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany and Finland found it in all of them, and because 01 to 05 and 09 cannot deliver it as they stand.
Prerequisite
A determination of not fit, or fit with restrictions to manage, from Process 05b.
Trigger
The case is opened. Then every certificate expiry.
Output
A suitable duties plan, agreed by the Patient and the Employer and revised against each new certificate.
Output
A closed case once capacity is restored and durable.
Rule
The case owns no calendar. The certificate's expiry is a date, and Process 08 is the only thing that watches dates. The case sets the date, hands it over, and stops until a determination comes back.
Rule
An unfit case means a Patient who is not fit, or fit only with restrictions, for the Role as it stands. Most of the volume is the second kind.
Rule
What crosses the wall is duties and dates. The Employer needs to know what the person may do and until when, and nothing else.
Rule
This process holds the state and makes the judgements. It knows what one Patient may do right now and whether that has settled. Process 08 knows when something is due. That is the whole difference between them.
Rule
Drawn for the Clinician. A Practitioner manages cases the same way inside their own Client organization.
Rule
The case carries the claim reference where there is one, so external documents tie together. Work Healthy Australia does not manage the claim, and the insurer is not a party to this process.
Rule
Whether a suitable duties plan is possible at all is a separate question from how it is drafted, and it is asked first. Where the Site has no duties that fit the measured restrictions, there is no plan to agree, and saying so is a different answer from an Employer who agreed and then did not implement. Conflating the two hides the case that needs the most attention.
Rule
Agreeing the duties is not the same as putting them in place. The case checks that the agreed duties are actually being worked, and where they are not, that is raised with the Employer and recorded as a barrier.
Rule
Barriers to recovery are recorded, and separately marked resolved. A case that will not close has a reason, and it is almost always here.
Rule
Advice is split by who it is for. Advice to the Employer, advice about the work, and advice for the Patient at home are three different things, and only the first crosses the wall.
Rule
Treatment is called from here and tracked, and it is not defined here. Work Healthy Australia refers, records attendance and progress, and re-assesses. What happens in a treatment session sits below this level, whoever delivers it.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers
