Process 05b: Provide A Service, render and determine

Prerequisite
A Patient admitted through 04a. Identity confirmed, consents recorded.
Trigger
A potentially open Role that a Patient needs to fill, whether through pre-employment screening or because an employee is not fit for their current role.
Output
An evaluation in one of three states, fit, fit with restrictions, or not fit, delivered to the Employer, with a mitigation plan where restrictions apply or the person is not fit. Per Patient.
Output
A determination that is not released here. Release, and the review before it, are Process 05c.
Rule
A restriction is measured, never asserted. Anything other than plain fit routes through functional testing against the Role requirements before the restrictions are recorded.
Rule
A safety gate sits before functional testing. Where an existing injury is disclosed, clearance comes first, because a lifting assessment can injure someone.
Rule
The determination records whether it is temporary or permanent, and a temporary one carries a review date. That date is what Process 08 reads.
Rule
The standing medical history is reviewed and updated at every visit. A longitudinal record nobody revisits is wrong within a year.
Rule
Consent to hold records, which Process 05a gates, is not consent to perform a procedure. Informed consent to the procedure is taken here, against what it involves, its risks and benefits, and the alternatives.
Rule
Drawn for the Clinician. A Practitioner renders the same way inside their own Client organization, and where the Site is outside it, no Patient or Service data is returned.
Rule
Documents belong to the record: the signed consent, an external certificate, an imaging report. Time spent is recorded against the service, because Process 09 bills time.
Rule
A standing alert on a Patient or a Client organization is shown when the record is opened. It is not an exception, it is a flag, and nothing clinical may be written into one, because it is read outside the wall.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Practitioners, Employers
