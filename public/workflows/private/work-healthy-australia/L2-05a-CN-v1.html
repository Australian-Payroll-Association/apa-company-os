L2 05a-CN: Confirm the consents

Sits under Process 05a, gate 2: Consent

Question it answers
How nine separate consents behave as one gate, and what happens when any single one is missing.
Answer
The required set is computed from the service, every one of them must be satisfied, and any single refusal ends the Service Order. There is no partial admission.
Rule
Consent is recorded against the person and the Client organization, not against the visit or the Service Order. That is what stops the Patient being asked the same thing on every visit. Later visits under that Client check what is held. They do not ask again.
Rule
The required set varies by service, the stored set does not. A pre-employment screen may require drug and alcohol consent where a complaint does not. So a person who has consented before can still have something outstanding, and it is only ever the difference that is asked for, never the whole set again.
Rule
Each consent is held against the version of the wording that was signed. When the wording changes, what was held against the old version is no longer current, and that one alone is asked again.
Rule
Refusing is a legitimate recorded outcome, not an error. The service does not proceed, and the Employer is told it was not completed, with no reason attached and no indication of which gate stopped it.
The nine
1. Take part in the assessment. 2. Collect and hold personal and health information. 3. Release the outcome to the Employer. 4. Contact the Patient's own treating doctor. 5. Drug and alcohol testing. 6. Receive survey invitations electronically. 7. Use de-identified data to improve the service. 8. Use data in research. 9. Informed consent to the procedure, which is taken in 05b because it is consent to an act rather than to holding a record.
In IMS
Storage exists for most of the set and the computation does not. `PatientConsent` holds it against an entity with a given date and a separate confirm date, which is the once-then-check pattern this process depends on. `ConsentSignature` carries privacy policy, electronic survey, research and drug and alcohol as separate grants, with a version and the signed file. `ConsentVerbal` is the procedure consent, and `PatientSurveyConsent` the survey one. `PESPatientDeclined` records a refusal. Six of the nine have a home. What is absent is the front of the process: nothing computes which consents a given service requires, so nothing can work out what is outstanding.
Roles
Patients, Clinicians, Employers
