How one mission runs

All four missions follow the same five steps. Only the task changes.

Repeats for every mission
  1. Open the mission from Mission Control. Learner
  2. Work the challenge with your AI Buddy in chat. Learner AI
  3. The deliverable is captured for you — no file to save. Automatic
  4. An AI grade card appears in the chat. AI
  5. Pass unlocks the next mission; below-pass reveals Retake. Automatic
Outcome one

Pass

  • The mission flips to mastered on the record.
  • The next mission unlocks.
  • A pass is final — it is never re-graded.
Outcome two

Not yet

  • The Retake button appears.
  • A retake is graded fresh from the top — a new, independent grade.
  • Never an edit of the old attempt.
AI grades everything

There is no human grader and no manual save. The platform captures each deliverable in the conversation and the AI writes the grade card the same way every time.

Applies to all four missions

How the loop holds

The rules

What always holds

  • One captured artifact per mission, from the learner's real work.
  • Grading checks completeness against the rubric — fully AI, no human.
  • The platform captures the deliverable in-conversation; no manual save.
  • A pass is final. Below-pass means Retake — a fresh, independent grade.
  • Missions unlock in order; the next opens only when the current is mastered.
The reasoning

Why it works

  • Each artifact builds on the last — mindset, data, brief, visuals.
  • The grade card is written by AI the same way every time — no subjective calls.
  • Real BOLT and team work means the output has value the day it's made.
  • Retake-not-resubmit keeps every grade honestly and independently earned.

Certification · runs once per learner

Gen AI certification

The credential certifies something that exists. When all four missions are mastered, the capstone unlocks; the learner ships it to production — not slides — the AI grader passes it, and a database trigger issues the certificate. The record and the certificate can never disagree.

Step by step

  1. Master all four missions. Certification requires every published Gen AI course, G01–G04, at mastered. No partial credit — three of four is locked, not "almost certified." Learner
  2. Capstone unlocks. The instant missionsCompleted === missionsTotal, the capstone flips locked → open. It fires on the fourth mastery — no one opens it by hand. Platform
  3. Ship the capstone to production. The final artifact deploys to production rather than a presentation. It builds on all four mission outputs — the portfolio proving itself in the real environment. Learner
  4. The capstone is graded. Graded for completeness against its rubric — fully AI, no human in the loop — resulting in passed or failed. An admin override exists for edge cases but clears the same bar. AI
  5. The trigger issues the certificate. On pass, trg_award_certification checks all four courses, writes earned_certification, the program flips to certified, and the Gen AI badge appears. DB trigger

To refine · open questions

A decision worth making

The flows above describe what runs today. This is a workflow-level decision worth making.

Capstone retake

Each mission has a clean pass / Retake loop. The capstone today is a soft resubmit (an overwrite warning), not a locked fail → Retake with defined attempts. Aligning the capstone to the same loop would make the whole track behave the same way end to end.
