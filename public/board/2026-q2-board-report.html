Three Highlights

1. Structural return to profit

+$73K swing to net income. The staff reduction cut contract labor 15% ($188.5K → $159.9K) while revenue grew. Cost down and top line up in the same quarter, which is why margin jumped 30 points.

2. AI Program breaks out

$4.5K → $37.9K (8.4×). Private retreats are taking off: Work Healthy, Australian Payroll, DOXA and Grady Golf all contributed. Our highest-value, least headcount-dependent line.

3. Staffing expands as the base

$206.6K (+10%) with ~48% contribution margin after direct VN cost. The reliable engine funding everything else. Top accounts: On Target, EO, Unlock, WareEase.

New: EO has verbally committed to more work on the events platform and other chapter-management tools, a proposed +$10K / month expansion.

Focus Area for Next Quarter

Reduce single-client concentration by scaling the high-margin AI Program

On Target alone was 52.5% of Q2 sales and the top 5 were 88%. That is the single biggest risk on the page: one account slipping would erase the profit we just recovered. The AI Program is the natural hedge, higher-margin, spread across many smaller clients, and already proving demand through the pipeline below. Q3 goal: grow AI Program toward parity in the mix and broaden staffing so no client exceeds ~40% of revenue.

Discussion Items

1. CEO Compensation effective H2 2026

  • Current: $36,000 / year, well below cost of replacement for the role.
  • Proposed: raise base to $120,000 / year, effective H2 2026 (not retroactive to H1).
  • Step-ups tied to annualized revenue, each sized at 10% of the growth between milestones: at $1.5M → $170K; at $2.0M → $220K (pre-ratified, automatic on trigger).
  • Cash impact: ~$42K incremental cost in H2, which draws on the same profit that funds the first dividend.

2. Dividend Policy first payout on H2 profit

  • Proposed: 50% of net profit as dividends, paid every six months, first distribution based on H2 2026 profit.
  • H1's $35,544 net profit is retained, not distributed. First payout is calculated on H2 net income at the close of H2.
  • H2 profit carries both the new $120K salary run-rate and the first 50% dividend, so the same pool serves both.
