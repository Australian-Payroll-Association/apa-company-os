The idea

Most of what you need, we have already built

Every business we work with arrives with the same shape of problem: data scattered across tools that don't talk to each other, reports assembled by hand, and no single place that answers a simple question quickly. The particulars differ. The shape does not.

So we stopped rebuilding it from scratch each time. We built Company OS, a proven company-database platform already running CRM, recruiting, HR, commerce, content, and reporting, the same system we run our own companies on. Roughly 80% of what you need is already there. The Jumpstart maps your data, stands up your own database, installs Company OS with the modules that fit you, and builds the first 2 to 4 automations that take real work off your team. What makes your business different, the other 20%, is where we spend the custom time from there, on a clear meter, only once the foundation is proven.

Money-back guarantee

Phase I  ·  weeks 1 to 4  ·  money-back gate

Map the data, agree the plan

We inventory every tool, spreadsheet, and inbox you rely on, and map each one to Company OS. This is the decision point. Give us the "go" and we start customising your database. If you are not satisfied with the plan instead, you get your money back.

2
Phase II  ·  weeks 4 to 8  ·  the build

Install Company OS, build the automations, hand it over

On "go", we customise your own database, install Company OS with the modules chosen in the plan, and rebuild the reports your team produces by hand so a board pack or a P&L becomes a question answered in minutes. From the scope uncovered in Phase I, we typically complete 2 to 4 automations or application customisations, the pieces that take the most repetitive work off your team. We finish with a training day so your people run the whole thing with Claude.
