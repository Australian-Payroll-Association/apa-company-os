The idea

Most of what you need, we have already built

Every business we work with arrives with the same shape of problem: data scattered across tools that don't talk to each other, reports assembled by hand, and no single place that answers a simple question quickly. The particulars differ. The shape does not.

So we stopped rebuilding it from scratch each time. We built Company OS, a proven company-database platform already running CRM, recruiting, HR, commerce, content, and reporting, the same system we run our own companies on. Roughly 80% of what you need is already there. The Jumpstart maps your data, stands up your own database, installs Company OS with the modules that fit you, and builds the first 2 to 4 automations that take real work off your team. What makes your business different, the other 20%, is where we spend the custom time from there, on a clear meter, only once the foundation is proven.

Risks & assumptions

The three that matter

1

If the 80% assumption doesn't hold for this business

The whole offer rests on roughly 80% of what you need already existing in Company OS. If your business is more bespoke than it looks, a fixed build would be wrong for both sides.

How we handle it

This is exactly what Phase I is for. The first four weeks buy a data map and a written plan before any build begins, and they are fully refundable. If the map shows a heavier job, the plan says so, and you can take the refund and stop there knowing exactly where you stand. The build only proceeds on a "go".

2

If the data is larger or messier than it looks from the outside

Data volume and quality are what move a build like this, and neither is visible until someone looks properly.

How we handle it

Phase I maps every source before the build starts, and anything beyond the standard scope, heavy migration, cleanup, specialist connectors, is metered Phase III work quoted in human tokens ($3,000 per 40-token pack) before it starts. Nothing lands as surprise hours inside the fixed fee; you always see it coming.

3

If the system is built but the team doesn't take it over

A database nobody runs is shelfware, whatever it cost.

How we handle it

The engagement is designed for handover from day one. You own the accounts outright, the training day puts your team at the controls with Claude, and the capability options, certification for your own people or an engineer alongside them, are there whenever you want them and never required. Edge8's aim is a client who doesn't need Edge8.
