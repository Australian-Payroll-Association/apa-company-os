The idea

Most of what you need, we have already built

Every business we work with arrives with the same shape of problem: data scattered across tools that don't talk to each other, reports assembled by hand, and no single place that answers a simple question quickly. The particulars differ. The shape does not.

So we stopped rebuilding it from scratch each time. The AI Program Jumpstart starts from a proven company-database template already running CRM, recruiting, HR, commerce, content, and reporting, the same system we run our own companies on. Roughly 80% of what you need is already there. We turn on the parts that fit your business, connect your existing tools, and hand you a system your team runs with Claude. What makes your business different, the other 20%, is where we spend the custom time, on a clear meter, only once the foundation is proven.

The opportunity

The question worth answering

If we could stand up the foundation for a fixed price you know up front, in 30 days, and structure it so the savings land in your pocket year after year, is that a project worth doing?

If the answer is yes, the rest is sequencing. Below is how the Jumpstart is staged so each step pays for itself before the next one begins.

The path

Three steps, each a standalone win

1
The audit

Map your data, then quote with certainty

A one-week engagement to inventory every tool, spreadsheet, and inbox you rely on, and map each one to the company-database model. You leave with a one-page data map and a firm, fixed quote for the Jumpstart. This is how we know your build is an 80% job before anyone commits to a price. The audit fee is credited in full against the Jumpstart if you proceed.

2
The jumpstart

Stand up the database, the connectors, and the reports

In 30 days we stamp your own company database from the template, turn on the domains that fit your business, wire in your standard tools, and rebuild the reports your team produces by hand today so a board pack or a P&L becomes a question answered in minutes. We finish with a training day so your team runs the whole thing with Claude. This is the out-of-the-box 80%, delivered at a fixed price.

3
The 20%

Build what makes your business different

With the foundation proven, we build the workflows unique to you: bespoke connectors to specialist tools, custom apps, retiring the software you no longer need. This is optional, metered, and paced by you. Buy a block of work when you want the next piece, and stop whenever it is far enough.
