The scope

8 weeks, two phases, one fixed fee

Money-back guarantee

The engagement is $15,000 AUD, paid up front. Weeks 1 to 4 are data mapping and a written implementation plan. If the Client is not satisfied with the plan, the full fee is refunded. Phase II proceeds only on the Client's "go".

1
Phase I  ·  weeks 1 to 4  ·  money-back gate

Map the data, agree the plan

Edge8 inventories every tool, spreadsheet, and inbox the Client relies on, and maps each one to Company OS. The Client leaves the first month with a one-page data map, a written implementation plan naming the modules and the 2 to 4 automations to be built, and the confirmed cost-of-today baseline. This is the decision point. On the Client's "go", Edge8 begins customising the database. If the Client is not satisfied with the plan, the full $15,000 is refunded.

2
Phase II  ·  weeks 4 to 8  ·  the build

Install Company OS, build the automations, hand it over

On "go", Edge8 customises the Client's own database, installs Company OS with the modules chosen in the plan, and rebuilds the reports the Client's team produces by hand today so a board pack or a P&L becomes a question answered in minutes. From the scope uncovered in Phase I, Edge8 typically completes 2 to 4 automations or application customisations. It finishes with a training day so the Client's team runs the whole thing with Claude.

The 20% · metered

Beyond the first automations

  • Automations or customisations past the 2 to 4 included
  • Connectors to specialist or industry tools
  • Custom workflows and apps built on the database
  • Migrating off, and retiring, the tools replaced

Bought in $3,000 blocks, on demand, once the foundation is live. The Client always sees it coming.

Running costs (the Client's own Supabase and Vercel, ~$70/month) and the ongoing options (AI Officer Certification, fractional or full-time engineer) are as per the proposal.

Roles & responsibilities

Who does what

Edge8

  • Runs Phase I data mapping and delivers the one-page data map, the written implementation plan, and the confirmed baseline
  • Honours the money-back guarantee: full refund if the Client is not satisfied with the Phase I plan
  • On "go", delivers Phase II: customises the database, installs Company OS with the modules chosen, wires the standard connectors, and builds the reporting layer
  • Completes 2 to 4 automations or application customisations scoped from Phase I
  • Runs the training day so the Client's team can operate the system with Claude
  • Builds metered 20% blocks when, and only when, the Client buys them

The Client

  • Pays the $15,000 fee up front, and gives the "go" (or requests the refund) at the end of Phase I
  • Brings the tools the business runs on, and roughly five reports the team would most want to run on demand
  • Agrees the cost-of-today baseline figures with Edge8 during Phase I
  • Owns the system and its accounts outright: the Supabase database, Vercel hosting, Claude usage, and their running costs
  • Runs the system with their own team after the training day, and decides if and when to buy 20% blocks or the ongoing options

Risks & assumptions

The three that matter

1

If the 80% assumption doesn't hold for this business

The whole offer rests on roughly 80% of what the Client needs already existing in Company OS. If this business is more bespoke than it looks, a fixed build would be wrong for both sides.

How we handle it

This is exactly what Phase I is for. The first four weeks buy a data map and a written plan before any build begins, and they are fully refundable. If the map shows a heavier job, the plan says so, and the Client can take the refund and stop there knowing exactly where they stand. The build only proceeds on a "go".

2

If the data is larger or messier than it looks from the outside

Data volume and quality are what move a build like this, and neither is visible until someone looks properly.

How we handle it

Phase I maps every source before the build starts, and anything beyond the standard scope, heavy migration, cleanup, specialist connectors, is metered 20% work quoted as a $3,000 block before it starts. Nothing lands as surprise hours inside the fixed fee; the Client always sees it coming.

3

If the system is built but the team doesn't take it over

A database nobody runs is shelfware, whatever it cost.

How we handle it

The engagement is designed for handover from day one. The Client owns the accounts outright, the training day puts the Client's team at the controls with Claude, and the capability options, certification for their own people or an engineer alongside them, are there whenever the Client wants them and never required. Edge8's aim is a Client who doesn't need Edge8.
