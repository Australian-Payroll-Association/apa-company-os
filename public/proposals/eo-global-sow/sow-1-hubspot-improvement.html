Already live in v1.0: New member acquisition Accelerator acquisition Lead routing & nurture

v1.0 put new member and accelerator acquisition into HubSpot. v1.1 adds the flows and the role views that let each chapter role run their part from the same place, plus the few pieces that keep members and sponsors from slipping. None of it is a heavy build.

Chapters not on HubSpot Not every chapter runs on HubSpot, and none are forced onto it. For those chapters, the global team tracks each application's stage inside the global HubSpot and the chapter is notified as it moves. Global gets full visibility; the chapter keeps working the way it does today. Moving a chapter fully onto HubSpot stays an option, never a requirement.

Chapter Manager

Runs the system day to day
The Monday view

One screen showing who's new, who's stuck, and what needs the manager's attention across every pipeline. Today that picture is spread across tabs, email, and a spreadsheet.

OutcomeThe chapter runs from one place, and nothing quietly stalls.
Global intake, routed

Applications enter through the global intake form and land in the right pipeline (member, SAP, or accelerator) for the right chapter, already sorted. For chapters not on HubSpot, the same intake is tracked and notified per the note above.

OutcomeNothing arrives unsorted; the manager works a clean queue.

Membership Chair

Qualifies and converts, owns renewals
Member renewal

Renewal reminders go out automatically, the chair gets a task for anyone who hasn't responded, and no renewal date passes unnoticed. Today it is sent by hand.

OutcomeRenewals run themselves; the chair handles only the exceptions.

Accelerator Chair

Owns accelerator acquisition and the handoff to membership
Accelerator pipeline & handoff

A prospect funnel for entrepreneurs in the $250k to $1M range, run like the member prospect funnel (prospect, qualified, applied, enrolled). When a participant graduates past $1M, they hand off automatically into the member prospect funnel, so the chapter never loses a graduate at the finish line.

OutcomeAccelerator growth feeds membership instead of leaking at graduation.

Member Engagement

Owns the new-member experience and executive programs
New member onboarding

A new member enters a structured first-months journey the moment they join, then hands off cleanly to the global 90-day program. This is the one addition that applies to every chapter the same way.

OutcomeNo cliff after the sale; every member gets the same start.
Key Executive pipeline

Members enroll their executives into EO's Key Executive programs, and it runs as a standard sales pipeline (prospect, qualified, enrolled) owned by the chapter. Executive interest is captured and worked like any other deal instead of sitting in a member's inbox.

OutcomeA repeatable path from "my exec might be interested" to enrolled, tracked end to end.

Marketing Chair

Fills the funnel and communicates out
Lead nurture campaigns

Automated nurture warms prospects by programme and chapter and hands them to the pipelines ready to work.

OutcomeThe funnel stays fed without manual chasing.
Communicate to the groups

Email members, moderators, and the board as defined groups, straight from HubSpot, without exporting lists to another tool. This is the area chapters are expected to grow into fastest.

OutcomeChapter-wide communication from the same system that holds the data.

SAP Chair

Owns sponsor relationships
SAP, new and renewal

The sponsor pipeline at both ends: acquisition from lead through to active partner, and renewal reminders ahead of each partner's expiry with tier kept current.

OutcomeNo sponsor quietly lapses, and the whole pipeline is visible.

Forum Chair

Owns forum placement and health
Forum view, synced from Altai

Forum rosters appear in HubSpot from the Altai sync (who is in which forum, current) without asking the site admin, and new-member placement happens here.

OutcomeForums are managed where everything else already lives.
Communicate to moderators

Message forum moderators as a group for training confirmations, check-ins, and updates.

OutcomeModerators stay in step without a separate mailing list.
