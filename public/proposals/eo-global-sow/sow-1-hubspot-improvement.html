Priority 1Retire the legacy CRM

Member and accelerator, application to conversion, end to end inside HubSpot and into Altai. These are the two flows that let EO sunset the legacy CRM, plus the pieces needed to stand them up cleanly. This is the priority; the rest waits behind it.

Member application to conversionMembership Chair

A member's application runs the whole way inside HubSpot: submitted, chapter and global approval, payment, and created as a member in Altai. Today this still lives in the legacy CRM. Finishing it here is half of what lets EO switch that system off.

OutcomeApplication to active member without the legacy CRM.
Accelerator application to conversion, with the Altai handoffAccelerator Chair

The same pipeline for entrepreneurs in the $250k to $1M band. The pipeline is already built; the missing piece is the handoff that submits an approved accelerator into Altai, exactly as members do. This is the other half of sunsetting the legacy CRM.

OutcomeAccelerator applicants convert and land in Altai, separated cleanly from members.
Usable intake form, embedded and automatedChapter Manager

The intake form finished to production quality, placed in the right chapter pages, collecting applicant data and creating the deal at the right stage automatically, assigned to the chapter and its staff owner.

OutcomeApplications enter as clean, owned deals from day one, no manual entry.
Pipeline clean-up and migrationSetup

Move the accelerator (EOA) deals sitting in HubSpot today into the accelerator pipeline, and set the correct user types across both pipelines, so member and accelerator are properly separated before the flows go live.

OutcomeBoth pipelines start clean and correctly typed.
Chair visibility fixChapter Manager

Make sure chairs can see the chapter's in-progress deals and create deals, a licensing and permission adjustment on the full licenses. Small, but it is blocking day-to-day use now.

OutcomeChairs see the whole chapter picture and can act on it.
Priority 2Light-lift role features

Agreed to be small builds with high value, most already built once at the regional level. Ordered roughly by the value the team voiced, and tagged by role.

Chapter dashboard, the Monday viewChapter Manager

One screen of action items: stalled deals, people needing attention, meetings to set. The chapter manager comes in and sees what is happening across the chapter, the way the regional instance did.

OutcomeThe chapter runs from one view; nothing stalls unseen.
Global intake, routed and notifiedChapter Manager / Global

Global leads route to the right chapter. On HubSpot they land there; off HubSpot the record is still held and the chapter is notified, then syncs back on chapter approval. Global tracks every lead either way.

OutcomeEvery global lead is routed and tracked, HubSpot chapter or not.
Member renewalMembership Chair

A renewal deal board with the steps along the way: an early check-in survey, the reminder as dues come due, and the option to assign chapter dues so the finance chair sees the renewal value coming.

OutcomeRenewals are tracked and forecastable, not sent by hand.
SAP, new and renewalSAP Chair

A sponsor prospect funnel, SAP nurture, and a renewal flow that follows the member-renewal pattern. When a new SAP chair comes in, the whole picture is already there. One of the highest-value adds from the regional build, used religiously in Vietnam.

OutcomeSponsors acquired, nurtured, and renewed, with no loss of continuity between chairs.
New member onboardingMember Engagement

A local onboarding flow that starts on payment and wraps around the global 30-day journey, with forum training folded in as part of onboarding rather than a separate track. Delivered with templates chapters can adapt, since this is the piece chapters customize most.

OutcomeA consistent first-months experience, customized per chapter, with no cliff after the sale.
Key Executive pipelineMember Engagement

Members enroll their executives into a chapter program (a leadership program, a learning academy), sold at the company level with the executives captured as key-exec contacts. It runs as a standard sale-plus-onboarding deal flow and doubles as a revenue generator and a retention tool. Data model to be confirmed together.

OutcomeExecutive programs run and communicated from HubSpot; members stay when their execs are engaged.
Nurture campaignsMarketing Chair

Automated nurture for prospects stuck in the pipe and for members, so prospects are not left cold, which is where most chapters fall down today.

OutcomeProspects and members get consistent touchpoints without manual chasing.
Forum view and moderator communicationForum Chair

A view-only forum board built from the Altai sync, given to chairs at no extra cost, plus the ability to email moderators as a group, one of the best ways to push chapter news, surveys, and event promotion.

OutcomeForums are visible where everything else lives, and moderators are one message away.
