The proposal below outlines the next set of automated flows recommended for EO Global's HubSpot instance. Each flow is described at the level of what changes for chapter staff and prospects, the underlying technical build will be scoped separately once each flow is confirmed.

1
Lead Qualification & Routing
New Lead → Sales Qualified Lead

When a new lead engages with a chapter, HubSpot automatically scores their level of interest and readiness. As that score crosses defined thresholds, the lead is promoted from Lead → Marketing Qualified → Sales Qualified, and the right chapter staff member is notified to follow up immediately. The follow-up task is tailored to what information is already on file: a call task when a phone number is available, or a request for contact details when it isn't.

OutcomeNo lead sits unactioned. Every qualified lead reaches a chapter staff member without anyone needing to monitor the pipeline manually.
2
Application → Deal Creation & Pipeline Routing
Application Submitted → Deal Created

When a prospect submits a membership application, HubSpot automatically creates the corresponding deal record and routes it into the correct pipeline, Member Acquisition or Accelerator Acquisition, based on the prospect's business revenue. No manual sorting is required by chapter staff, and every application is captured consistently regardless of which chapter it comes through.

OutcomeEvery submitted application becomes a trackable deal in the right pipeline, automatically, the moment it's submitted.
3
Stage-Based Nurture Communications
Connect & Qualify → Formal Interview / Go–No-Go

As a prospect's application moves through each stage of chapter and global review, the right message is sent automatically, keeping the prospect informed and engaged without chapter staff needing to remember to follow up manually at every step.

OutcomeConsistent, timely communication with every prospect, regardless of chapter staff bandwidth.
4
Member Onboarding Pipeline
Closed Won → Active Onboarding

Once a deal closes as won, the new member is automatically handed into a dedicated Member Onboarding pipeline, a structured, staged track that guides the new member through their first weeks, rather than relying on chapter staff to remember a manual first-touch follow-up. This replaces ad-hoc welcome outreach with a consistent, no-gaps onboarding experience from day one of membership.

OutcomeEvery new member gets the same high-quality onboarding experience, with visibility for chapter staff into where each new member stands in that journey.
Scope note

The Member Onboarding Pipeline itself does not yet exist in the current build, this is new scope, not a reuse of an existing pipeline, and will need its own stage design as part of this build.

5
Strategic Alliance Partner (SAP) Renewal Pipeline & Automation Acquisition already built
Active Partner → Renewal Window → Renewed

The SAP Acquisition pipeline already exists in the global portal. The renewal side does not, this flow proposes building the SAP Renewal pipeline and the automation behind it: partner tier stays in sync automatically between the partner's company record and their deal, and a renewal reminder is triggered ahead of each partner's expiration date so no renewal is missed.

OutcomePartner renewals are tracked with the same rigor as membership, with tier and renewal timing always current, closing the gap left by the acquisition-only build.
Scope note

Unlike Flow 4, this is not automation added to an existing pipeline, the SAP Renewal pipeline itself is new scope and will need its own stage design, alongside the tier-sync and reminder automation described above.

6
Podcast Engagement Pipeline
Podcast Registration → Prospect Record

When someone registers for an EO podcast, HubSpot automatically sends an acknowledgment and creates a prospect record, capturing engaged listeners as trackable leads from the moment they register, without chapter staff needing to manually follow up on registration lists.

OutcomePodcast listeners become a captured, trackable audience the moment they engage, ready for chapter follow-up.
Scope note

Before this is finalized, we'll confirm whether the podcast program runs globally across chapters or is specific to individual chapters, this determines whether the pipeline is built once at the global level or offered as a chapter-optional add-on.

7
Rise Program Follow-Up Pipeline
Rise Event Attended → Membership Package Outreach

After a prospect attends a Rise event, HubSpot automatically runs a structured follow-up sequence introducing the membership package, keeping momentum from the event without relying on chapter staff to manually schedule follow-up outreach.

OutcomeEvery Rise attendee receives timely, consistent follow-up while their interest is highest.
Scope note

Before this is finalized, we'll confirm whether Rise runs as a global EO program (making this a standard pipeline across chapters) or is chapter-specific like the podcast program above, and whether Rise attendance should route into the existing Member or Accelerator Acquisition pipelines or remain standalone.

Next step

Once the flows above are confirmed, each will be scoped into a detailed technical workflow specification for build.
