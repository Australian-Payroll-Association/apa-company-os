Global membership approval, HubSpot to Altai

Build the global membership approval flow that runs after chapter approval: a prospect enters through HubSpot, the global team approves, the member pays through Chargebee, and a member record is created in Altai. This is the piece the legacy migration depends on.

For clarity. The four-step chapter approval is effectively done. This SOW covers the four-step global approval — the part that isn't built and that moving off legacy depends on. Running it in HubSpot lets the global team manage any chapter from one place, whether or not that chapter has its own HubSpot instance.
01

Objective

Replace the legacy submission path with a HubSpot-native global approval flow that ends in a paid member and a member record in Altai — the mechanism that retires legacy for global membership.
