What we heard

Where you are today

You run a bricks-and-mortar retail business with an ecommerce arm, around 160 staff, mostly in stores, with about 20 in the back office. Your team is further along with AI than most:

  • LLMs are in daily use and you already have some agentic workflows running.
  • Two quarters of groundwork done. You documented the workflows in a few key departments to find the bottlenecks worth fixing.
  • Leadership is trained, the rest of the team isn't. AI capability so far has reached the leadership group and stopped there.

And then it stalled. There's no in-house technical skillset to take the next step, and everyone is too stretched to self-teach. New projects meet resistance because the team is already under-resourced. In your words, you've done it a little backwards: the bottlenecks are mapped, but nobody has the time or the skills to act on the findings.

The opportunity

The question worth answering

What if the team you already have could get meaningfully more done without adding headcount, because the training finally reaches your store managers and back office, and a central database lets AI do the heavy lifting?

That's the goal you described, and the good news is the hard part is already done. The workflow documentation you spent two quarters on is exactly the input a database design and automation roadmap needs. What's missing is the capability to execute, and that's what the two tracks below deliver, in parallel, from August.

The path

Two tracks, starting together in August

1
Track one · The capability

AI Officer certification for 30–40 of your people

We put your store managers and back-office team through the Edge8 AI Officer certification platform. It's challenge-based, not attendance-based: every person builds real automations on real work and has proof of what they can do at the end. This is how AI capability cascades past the leadership team, without pulling anyone out of their day job for weeks of classroom time.

2
Track two · The foundation

Central database & automation roadmap

A consulting engagement where we map your current systems and data, design your central company database, and turn your workflow documentation into a prioritised automation roadmap. Then we start building, beginning with the bottlenecks your own mapping already identified, so each automation gives time back to a team that needs it.

3
Optional follow-on

A dedicated AI engineer

Once the roadmap is set and the first automations are live, you can add a dedicated Edge8 engineer (based in Vietnam, working as part of your team) to keep building. Your certified people know what to ask for; the engineer ships it. This is optional and only makes sense after the foundation is in place.
