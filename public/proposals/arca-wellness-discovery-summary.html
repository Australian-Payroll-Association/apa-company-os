What Justin wants out of the retreat

Notes from the discovery call with Justin Cohen, founder of Arca Wellness, ahead of the 1-day Infinite Leverage Private Retreat.

ClientJustin Cohen, Arca Wellness
Call withDave Hajdu, Edge8
DateAugust 12, 2026
Engagement1-Day Private Retreat, up to 8 people

Context

Where Arca Wellness is today

Arca Wellness is an early-stage wellness e-commerce brand on Shopify (product line includes Arca Max). The numbers are still small and the data still clean: roughly one order in, and a launch waitlist of about 150 people collected in Brevo. The team is small and coordinates over WhatsApp, with a possible move to Slack.

The commercial thesis matters for everything below: Justin expects most sales to come through partners and distribution channels (retail chains, tech companies, healthcare), not the brand site. A competitor confirmed the same pattern. So polishing the storefront is a nice-to-have; the systems behind the partner pipeline and the team are the real gap.

Justin's own ranking

"If I would put priorities: be more efficient with my company and my team, and putting that on the database, obviously would be my first choice."

What he wants

Five things, in order

1
First priority

A central company database he owns

Today data is scattered across Shopify, Brevo, WhatsApp, and one-off Claude dashboards (a "mission control" that went stale because the team can't share it). He wants one database everyone works from, with Shopify and Brevo synced into it rather than living beside it.

2
Second priority

Partner and sales pipeline tracking

Many partner conversations are live right now with no system behind them: no lead status, no follow-ups, no revenue potential or cost view. Contracts are untracked (which are signed, which are pending) and documents have no home. This is the CRM he actually needs.

3
Third priority

Team management without micromanagement

Justin works 4am to 10pm and still chases updates by hand. He wants task boards per person, daily check-ins that write themselves, goals the whole team can see, and approvals that come to him instead of him going to everyone. The WhatsApp-chat-as-task-tracker era ends here.

4
Fourth priority

Automated customer replies and marketing

Purchase questions and product questions ("what is the Arca Max?") arrive on WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram and are answered manually. He was evaluating respond.io-type tools; the better path is automatic replies that know the company because they sit on the database, with Brevo and Resend as the send engines.

5
Fifth priority

Learn the stack well enough to self-serve

He's an ex-developer (PHP, some SQL) who is comfortable building with Claude but lost on agents, workflows, and the modern stack. He wants to understand GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase well enough that he and the team keep building after we leave, including Shopify theme changes by CLI without a web developer.

Agreed scope

What the retreat day covers

Morning

  • Install the developer stack: Homebrew, GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Shopify CLI, Brevo, Resend
  • Wire the accounts together so Claude can reach everything
  • Prove it: pull the Shopify theme via CLI and ship a live change

Afternoon

  • Install the Edge8 company database template into Justin's own Supabase
  • Clone a fresh repository into his GitHub (handle: sofresh) with a playground site
  • Practice the method: plan first, then build, with a partner CRM as the learning project

Explicitly out of scope for the day: redesigning the storefront. Justin can iterate on look and feel himself once the CLI access is in place.

Beyond the day

What this can grow into

  • Full data setup. If he wants the Shopify and Brevo syncs and first workflows built for him, that's a separate engagement, scoped after the retreat. Arca is a light case: few data sources, almost no historical data to migrate.
  • Slack over WhatsApp. A likely early move, since Slack integrates cleanly with the AI stack he's adopting.
  • A second company. Justin's wife runs a much larger company with none of this in place. If the retreat lands, he wants to make that introduction.
