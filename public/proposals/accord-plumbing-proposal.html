The investment

One build, two environments, your choice

Same scope either way. The difference is where it lives and what it costs to build and run.

Option A: Supabase + Vercel

A$15,000 fixed

Our core stack: a Postgres database on Supabase with hosting on Vercel, built on top of AWS and hostable in Australia. It's the architecture we recommend for medium-sized companies, and the one Edge8 runs its own business on. Cheapest to build, cheapest to run (roughly A$100/month), and the easiest for you to maintain and extend yourself. Your data, your accounts, fully exportable Postgres. Our recommendation.

Option B: Native Azure

A$25,000 fixed

The same foundation built where you already are: Azure SQL and Azure App Service in your own Microsoft tenant, alongside SharePoint and 365. Azure is optimized for large enterprise, which is why it costs more here and after: expect ongoing running costs typically 50% to 75% higher than Option A, and a platform that is harder to maintain on your own.

Both prices are in AUD and fixed for the full foundation scope above. Hosting runs in your own accounts either way; you own everything from day one.
