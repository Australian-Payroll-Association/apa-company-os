What we heard, and how we solve it

Your questions, answered

01

How will we learn to build and make updates?

Our solution

You get admin tools to update your own site, and you begin learning how to use Claude Code within your stack. To accelerate that, you can come to Vietnam for an Infinite Leverage Retreat.

02

We are not technical.

Our solution

We do the heavy lifting. We build a production-grade application and configure your full stack. Over 90 days you learn to build workflows and turn them into new features for your clients, leaning on our team's human expertise to harden the code and make sure it is production ready.

03

We are not a startup. This is an extension of our business.

Our solution

Understood, and we build for exactly that. The new application is built in parallel while you keep working with what you have today. We onboard one customer first, then all of them. Once customers are on the platform, every change follows a strict Build, then Stage & Review, then Deploy process. A standard SDLC (Software Delivery Life Cycle).

04

Are you throwing away our prototypes?

Our solution

No. We fully leverage them. Your prototypes become the blueprint we use to write the production-quality code.

05

Our business is complex.

Our solution

We are confident here. We have handled complex problems at the highest levels. The complexity gets solved through a series of conversations and by exploring your prototypes. It gets better along the way, because your tribal knowledge is documented as we go.

06

Different customers need different data fields.

Raised by Kevin
Our solution

Agreed. We build a data model that solves 80% up front, and as needs change we use Claude to extend it. This is exactly why it matters to have people with a solid database design background on the build.
