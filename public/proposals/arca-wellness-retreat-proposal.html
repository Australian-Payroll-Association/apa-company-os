What we heard

Efficiency first, website second

Arca Wellness is a wellness e-commerce brand on Shopify, early enough that the data is still clean: one order in, a 150-person launch waitlist in Brevo, and a small team coordinating over WhatsApp. Sales will come mostly through retail, tech, and healthcare partners rather than the site itself, and that partner pipeline is the part of the business with no system behind it today.

  • No central database. Data lives in Shopify, Brevo, WhatsApp, and one-off dashboards the team can't share or update.
  • Founder overload. 4am to 10pm days, manual check-ins, micromanagement by necessity rather than choice.
  • Untracked pipeline. Partner conversations, contract status, and documents have no system of record.
  • Manual replies. Purchase and product questions on WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram are all answered by hand.
  • Stack unknown. GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase are new territory, and that gap blocks everything above.

Your words on the call: efficiency with the company and the team, on a database you own, is the first choice. The website look and feel can follow. This retreat is built exactly to that ranking. The full discovery summary is here: what Justin wants from the retreat →

The plan

One day, three moves

1
Morning  ·  the stack

Install the developer stack

Homebrew and the CLIs, then accounts wired together: GitHub (handle: sofresh), Vercel, Supabase, Shopify CLI, Brevo, and Resend. About 90 minutes to a working environment where Claude can reach everything through one set of keys.

2
Midday  ·  the proof

Make a live change to your Shopify site

Pull your theme with the Shopify CLI, have Claude modify it, and push it live. From that moment your team can adjust the storefront by asking, without waiting on a web developer.

3
Afternoon  ·  the foundation

Your company database, plus a playground

We install the Edge8 company database template into your own Supabase account and clone a fresh repository into your GitHub, with a playground site to practice on. You leave with the same foundation we run Edge8 on: yours to extend, with a CRM as your first learning project.
