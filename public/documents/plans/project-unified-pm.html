01

What it is

Three questions, answered at any moment.

  • What are we working on? Projects and their stage.
  • Where are our people? Who is on what, and their workload.
  • What is each job costing us? Hours logged times each person’s cost rate, against budget.

Everything hangs off one loop: create a project, log time, cost rolls up against budget. Each team keeps its own project types and fields under one roof, rather than one forced structure.

The rule that keeps costs honest

Never store cost on a row. Work out hours × current rate when you read it. Storing it creates two truths and breaks the moment a rate changes.

05

Decisions to settle

Policy and definitions, not code. Settle them early so nothing gets built twice.

Decision
Who is “leadership”. The cost gate needs a list of who may read rates and project cost. Uses the existing can_view_sensitive permission, so it is a setting, not a build.
Decision
One source of truth for hours. The tasks table already has an hours proxy; reconcile it with the new time_entry so cost is worked out from one place.
Watch
Adoption is make or break. Build the timesheet and the fast logging screen first. Slow logging means no logging, and then every cost number is wrong.
